The Porto do Funchal has been elected Best Cruise Terminal in the World for Sustainability 2026 by the World Cruise Awards. This recognizes the Madeira Port Administration's work in sustainability, digitalization, and energy transition.

This marks the second time Madeira has received this accolade, having previously won in 2024. The award was presented at the annual World Cruise Awards ceremony held in Jamaica.

> "Congratulate all the workers of the Madeira port community for the distinction of Porto do Funchal as Best Cruise Terminal in the World in terms of Sustainability 2026. This international recognition is a source of pride for the Region and, above all, a tribute to the professionalism, dedication and commitment of all those who contribute daily to the quality of our ports and to the excellence of welcoming those who visit us."
