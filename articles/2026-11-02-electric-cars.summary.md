# Electric Cars Make Up Almost a Third of New Cars in Portugal This Year

More than 30% of the new passenger cars registered in Portugal between January and October are **fully electric**. In October alone, they made up almost half of the market.

The figures were published on Monday by the Automobile Association of Portugal (**ACAP**) and reported by the news agency Lusa. They cover the whole country and do not give a separate figure for Madeira.

## Fully Electric Cars Take 30.3% of 205,507 New Registrations

A total of **205,507 passenger cars** were registered in Portugal in the first ten months of the year. Battery electric vehicles, known as BEVs, were the largest group among cars with alternative power, at 30.3%.

Hybrids followed with 25.4%, and **plug-in hybrids** with 15.1%.

## Only 22.5% of New Cars Run Solely on Petrol or Diesel

Cars powered only by **petrol** made up 19.1% of registrations, and diesel cars just 3.4%. ACAP puts the remaining 77.5% in its alternative energy group, which includes electric cars and all types of hybrids.

## Battery Electric Sales Grow 50.5%, With Tesla Selling 9,673 Cars

Registrations of fully electric cars grew **50.5%** compared with the first ten months of 2025.

**Tesla** was the best-selling brand, with 9,673 cars, up 62.5%. BMW came second with 5,552 (up 47.8%) and Mercedes-Benz third with 4,424 (up 89.1%).

## October Brings 7,452 Electric Registrations, 44.9% of the Market

The trend was even stronger last month. In **October**, 7,452 fully electric cars were registered, 61.4% more than in the same month a year earlier. That was 44.9% of all new passenger cars.

## Hybrid Registrations Rise 21.1% to 52,192, Led by Peugeot

Hybrids grew 21.1% in the first ten months, to **52,192 cars**. The French brand Peugeot led with 9,443 (up 12.6%), ahead of Toyota with 8,466 (up 21.8%) and Citroën with 3,986 (up 125.5%).

October was weaker. Hybrid registrations **fell 9.3%** to 2,761 cars, or 16.6% of the market.

## Plug-In Hybrids Reach 30,948 Cars but Fall 19.2% in October

Plug-in hybrids, or PHEVs, grew 9.9% to **30,948 cars**. German brands dominate this group: Mercedes-Benz sold 6,631 (down 7.2%), BMW 4,666 (down 9.5%) and Volkswagen 2,922 (up 24.2%).

In October, PHEV registrations **fell 19.2%**, giving them a market share of 15.8%.

In Madeira, 1,012 of the nearly 3,800 new cars registered in 2025 were fully electric, Times of Madeira [reported earlier this year](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/44-of-all-2025-car-registrations-in-madeira-were-electrified). Demand for public support is also high: the national **electric car subsidy** for private buyers [ran out within hours](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/electric-car-subsidy-runs-out-within-hours-on-opening-day) on its opening day.
