# KdF Visitors Welcomed in Madeira, 1935-1939

Between 1935 and 1939, Portuguese media documented the arrival of thousands of German KdF visitors. **Portuguese media** extensively covered these organized cruises to Madeira, noting the significant numbers choosing the island as their destination.

:::callout 📰
**Historical Context**: The KdF (Kraft durch Freude) program was Nazi Germany's state-operated leisure organization that provided affordable vacations and travel opportunities to German workers.
:::

Observers noted their conduct was **"order, discipline and joy"**. They were described as **"fat ladies, strong men"** who visited cities **"in dense groups"** and frequented **"cafés and breweries"**.

Since **"Germany sends most tourists"**, the KdF program was considered **"best propaganda agent"**. Some dedicating themselves **"more to pleasure travel"** through these visits.

Returning workers recalled their time in Portugal as **"full of warmth"** and feeling **"vibrant with welcome"** from the Atlantic journey experience.
