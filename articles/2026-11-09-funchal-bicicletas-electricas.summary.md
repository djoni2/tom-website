# Funchal Tests 15 Electric Bike Prototypes With Students

Funchal's city council and the University of Madeira have joined a European research project to trial **15 prototype electric bicycles** with students. The bikes are 100% electric and built with recyclable materials, the council said in a statement.

The scheme falls under In-Mob, a project coordinated by the **University of Catania** in Italy. It aims to introduce shared micromobility schemes built around electric bikes and charging infrastructure.

## Protocol Signed at Baltazar Dias Theater on November 9

City officials signed the protocol this morning at the **Teatro Municipal Baltazar Dias**. Funchal's city council, known as the CMF, was represented by vice-president Carlos Rodrigues.

The goal is to gather data on how the electric bikes perform in real conditions. That data should help shape future **sustainable mobility policies** for the island.

## Funchal Picked for Its Steep Terrain, Palermo for Transit Links

Funchal and Palermo, in Italy, were chosen as the project's two pilot cities. Funchal was picked for its **challenging topography**, while Palermo was chosen for how well it integrates bikes with public transport.

> In-Mob aims to test the performance of motorised bicycles in areas with steep slopes, assessing how well people accept this mode of transport as an energy-efficient and environmentally sustainable way to travel on short trips.

**Funchal City Council**, in a statement

## Six-Month Trial Links Student Residence to University Campus

Students living at the university residence will be able to use the bikes for **six months**. They will ride them on trips between the residence and the University of Madeira.

## Charging Station With Solar Panels Set for Rua De Santa Maria

The 15 bikes come with a charging infrastructure powered by **photovoltaic panels**. It will be installed at the university residence on Rua de Santa Maria, where the students live.

## Funchal City Council Receives €144,000 in EU Funding

By joining the In-Mob project, Funchal's city council secured **€144,000 in funding**.

## In-Mob Project Started in 2023 With Eleven Partners

In-Mob is short for Innovative Products for Sustainable Micromobility. The project began in **2023** and brings together eleven partners to develop and test new micromobility products.
