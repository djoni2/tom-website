# Housing Rights Rally Fills Funchal's Largo do Município

Residents gathered at Largo do Município in Funchal on Saturday, November 14, to demand **urgent action** on Madeira's housing crisis. The Plataforma Casas para Viver organized the rally.

The group said the protest answers to no political party. Organizers described it instead as a fight for **human rights** and the right to housing.

## Three Spokespersons Frame the Fight as a Human Rights Issue

Diogo Teixeira, Liliana Valente and Mariana Pires spoke for the platform. They said the gathering brought together residents who face, or have faced, difficulty finding a home, alongside **supporters** of the cause.

Organizers named the **growth of short-term rentals**, rising rents, unaffordable costs and the struggle to buy a home as the main pressures behind the crisis.

> Housing is a right, speculation is not.

**The spokespersons**

The spokespersons warned that Madeira is becoming **less affordable** for the people who live there.

## March Aims to Reach the Assembleia Legislativa da Madeira

The platform plans to carry its message to the **Assembleia Legislativa da Madeira**, the regional parliament. It is demanding concrete answers from the Regional Government and other political leaders.

Organizers argued that public policy keeps favoring **speculative interests** over the needs of residents. They said this makes lasting solutions hard to reach.

## Movement Echoes Protests in Spain and France

The Funchal gathering also voiced solidarity with similar movements across Portugal and in other European countries, naming **Spain and France**.
