# Madeira's 500 Biggest Companies Grew Sales 7.6% in 2025

Madeira's 500 largest companies raised their turnover by **7.6%** in 2025. That was faster than the 5.9% growth of all companies in the region and the 5.3% growth across Portugal.

Together, the 500 companies had a turnover of **about €4.4 billion**. They employed more than 29,000 people and created about €1.5 billion in gross value added.

Their **sales to foreign markets** reached €213 million.

The figures were presented by **António Barreiros**, commercial director of Informa D&B. The company is the knowledge partner of the "500 Biggest and Best Companies in Madeira" initiative.

## Sales Rose 78.8% Between 2021 and 2025

The study shows the growth did **not start last year**. Between 2021 and 2025, the combined turnover of the 500 biggest companies grew by 78.8%.

Over the same period, turnover grew by **56.7% for all companies** in Madeira and by 36.7% in Portugal as a whole.

The figures are at **current prices**, so they are not adjusted for inflation. For the biggest companies, they mean average growth of close to 16% a year.

## Net Margin Slipped to 18.5% but Stayed High

In 2025, the net margin of the **500 biggest companies** was 18.5%. All Madeira companies had a net margin of 15.5%, and the national average was 7.6%.

In other words, the biggest companies in the region made about **€18.50 in net profit** for every €100 of turnover, taken together.

The margin did **fall from 19.5%** in 2024. Return on shareholders' funds also dropped, from 17.3% to 15.6%. It still stayed above the regional figure of 11.5% and the national figure of 9.4%.

## Industry Sales Rose 22.7%, Real Estate Fell 3.4%

Sales growth was **very different between sectors**. Industry had the biggest rise, at 22.7%, followed by retail, at 21.4%.

Wholesale trade grew 13.9%, **general services 11.9%** and construction 8.7%.

Two sectors went the other way. Real estate activities **fell by 3.4%**, while information and communication technology saw a small drop of 0.2%.

## Four Sectors Hold About Two-Thirds of the Companies

Barreiros pointed to the weight of **four areas of activity**: retail, accommodation and food services, construction, and wholesale trade.

Together, these four areas account for about **two-thirds** of the 500 companies, and for about two-thirds of their turnover.

## Eleven Companies Report Turnover Above €50 Million

The study found **11 companies** with turnover above €50 million. It found 51 companies with at least 100 workers.

About 61% of the companies have been in business for **over 20 years**. One in five has a family management structure.

## Only 12% of the Companies Do Business Abroad

International business **remains limited**. Only 12% of the companies do business in foreign markets.

Foreign markets make up **4.8%** of the combined turnover of the 500 biggest companies.

## About 77% Show High or Medium-High Financial Resilience

Informa D&B also looked at **financial strength**. It concluded that about 77% of the companies it assessed show high or medium-high levels of resilience.

About 37% received **high or medium-high ratings** on environmental, social and governance (ESG) indicators.
