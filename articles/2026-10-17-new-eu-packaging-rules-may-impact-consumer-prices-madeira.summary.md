# New EU Packaging Rules May Impact Consumer Prices in Madeira

New European packaging regulations will require businesses to **redesign materials and processes**, potentially impacting consumer prices. The PPWR regulation entered into force on 12 August with phased implementation.

## Changes Coming in Phases

From 2027, Madeira's hotels, restaurants and cafés must allow customers to **bring their own containers** for takeaway food. By 2028, these establishments must also offer reusable container options and ensure tea bags are compostable.

By 2030, individual packaging for items like **sauce, sugar and salt** will be banned in restaurants. The regulation aims to reduce waste, promote reuse and improve recyclability across Madeira's business sector.

Industry associations warn the changes represent a "structural" and "complex" transition requiring significant investment in new equipment, materials and systems. However, consumer protection group DECO stresses sustainability costs must be **shared across supply chains**, not passed disproportionately to consumers.

:::callout
**Context**: The PPWR represents one of the most significant updates to EU packaging law in decades, affecting everything from material selection to end-of-life processing.
:::

The regulation also includes requirements for **labelling standards**, recycled content incorporation, and reducing empty space in packaging. Companies across Madeira's hospitality, retail, and manufacturing sectors will need to adapt their operations to comply with these evolving standards over the coming years.
