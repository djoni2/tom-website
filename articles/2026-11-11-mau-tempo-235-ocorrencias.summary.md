# Storm Charles Triggers 235 Incidents Across Madeira

Madeira's Civil Protection service logged **235 weather-related incidents** across the Region by 11:00 on Wednesday. The count covers the period since 20:00 the night before, as Storm Charles moved through the archipelago. It follows Storm Bruna, which struck the island earlier in the same spell of bad weather.

The figures come from the Preventech platform, cited in the civil protection service's report to the press. Responding crews mobilized **603 personnel** and 244 vehicles in total. Those numbers count each deployment per incident, not unique staff or vehicles, so the same team can be counted more than once across different calls.

## Mass Movements and Flooding Each Hit 64 Times

Landslides and other mass movements were the most frequent type of incident, with **64 occurrences**. Flooding of structures or surfaces matched that figure exactly, also at **64 occurrences**. Fallen trees followed, with **36 registered incidents**.

Civil Protection also recorded **eight collapses** of built structures, five falls of construction elements and five blocked drains or pipes. Damage to or falls of utility networks accounted for seven cases, and three incidents involved temporary or mobile structures falling over.

## Funchal Logged 54 Incidents, the Most of Any Council

Funchal recorded the highest number of incidents, with **54 calls**. Civil Protection sent 136 firefighters and 56 vehicles to respond across the capital.

Câmara de Lobos and Machico tied for second place, each with **32 incidents**. Câmara de Lobos drew 88 firefighters and 31 vehicles, while Machico saw 97 firefighters and 33 vehicles deployed, despite the lower incident count.

## Ponta do Sol Reports 17 Incidents, Calheta Eight

Ponta do Sol logged **17 incidents**, backed by 53 firefighters and 19 vehicles. Calheta followed with **eight incidents**, supported by 23 firefighters and nine vehicles.

Porto Santo registered **two incidents**, with six firefighters and two vehicles sent. Porto Moniz had just **one incident**, handled by two firefighters and one vehicle. Eight further incidents were not attributed to any specific council.

## Over 200 Calls Reported in Every Council

Storm Charles followed Storm Bruna, and the back-to-back systems brought a night of **heavy rain** across every council in Madeira and Porto Santo. Civil Protection's report describes more than **two hundred incidents** spread across the entire archipelago, with no council left untouched.

**[Regional Government officials reported](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/storm-charles-more-rain-than-2010-flood)** earlier Wednesday that Storm Charles dropped more rain in 12 hours than the storm behind the island's deadly 2010 flood. This time, no serious injuries were reported.
