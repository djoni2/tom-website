# Praia Formosa Promenade to Stay Shut for Two More Weeks

The promenade at Praia Formosa will **remain closed** to pedestrians for about two more weeks. The Sociedades de Desenvolvimento said the shutdown follows damage from storm Charles.

## Decision Follows First Site Inspection at Praia Formosa

In a statement, the Sociedades de Desenvolvimento said the decision to **extend the closure** came after an initial inspection on site. That first check found the need for a **deeper review** of the structure's safety and of any damage the bad weather may have caused.

## Funchal Path Was Already Closed During Storm's Peak

The route had already been **shut as a precaution** during the period of worst weather instability. Even so, officials said conditions are **still not in place** to let people walk the path safely.

## Praia Formosa Stays Shut Until Checks Finish

Reopening depends on **finishing the technical assessments** and on any repairs those checks show are needed. Pedestrian access stays **banned across the area** covered by the closure order. The Sociedades de Desenvolvimento asked the public to respect the signs and access restrictions.

The closure follows storm Charles, which **[flooded streets, trapped families](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/storm-charles-traps-families-floods-streets-madeira)** across Madeira earlier this week.
