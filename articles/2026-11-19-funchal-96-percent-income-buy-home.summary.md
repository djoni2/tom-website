# Funchal Needs 96% of Income to Buy a Home

Funchal is the Portuguese city where families face the **biggest financial strain** to buy a home. A mortgage payment there takes up **96% of family income**, according to an idealista analysis published Wednesday, covering the third quarter of 2026.

The same study ranks Funchal as Portugal's **most pressured rental market**, third overall. Renting there absorbs **85% of family income**, behind only Faro (92%) and Lisbon (88%).

## Renters in Funchal Pay 85% of Income, Third-Highest

Among the 20 cities idealista analyzed, Funchal ranks third for rental pressure. Faro leads the table at **92%**, followed by Lisbon at 88% and then Funchal.

Porto comes next, **well behind at 66%**. Setúbal follows at 64%, then Ponta Delgada at 56% and Aveiro at 54%.

## National Averages Trail at 70% for Buying, 83% for Renting

Nationally, the effort rate stands at **70% for buying** a home and **83% for renting** one, idealista says. Funchal exceeds both figures by a wide margin.

The gap is widest in home buying. Funchal's 96% rate sits **26 percentage points above** the national average for a mortgage.

## Lisbon Ranks Second for Buying Pressure at 90%

Funchal tops the 20-city ranking for home-buying pressure. Lisbon follows in second place at **90%**, then Faro at 79% and Porto at 76%.

**Aveiro posts 68%**, ahead of Ponta Delgada and Setúbal at 59% and 57%. Braga and Viana do Castelo both sit at 53%, Leiria at 51%, Coimbra at 47%, Évora and Viseu at 45%, and Santarém at 43%.

## Guarda Has Portugal's Lowest Burden at Just 20%

At the other end of the ranking, Guarda posts the **lowest home-buying effort rate**, at 20%. Castelo Branco and Portalegre follow, both at 23%.

Bragança sits at 26%, Beja at 29% and Vila Real at 30%. These **six cities trail** the national table.

## Only Six Cities Meet the 33% Affordability Limit for Buying

idealista flags **33% of income** as the recommended ceiling for housing costs. Just **six district capitals** meet that limit for buying a home.

Renting is even harder to afford. Only **three cities** stay under the same threshold: Castelo Branco and Portalegre at 32%, and Guarda at 29%.

## Analysis Covers the Third Quarter of 2026

idealista's effort-rate calculation draws rental prices and home values from its own property listings. Income figures come from **Portugal's National Statistics Institute**, INE.

The mortgage calculation was **recently updated** using interest-rate data from the European Central Bank. It factors in average loan duration and typical interest rates for a mortgage.
