# Portugal's October Was Driest in 40 Years, IPMA Says

Mainland Portugal just had its driest **October in 40 years**. That is the finding of a climate bulletin released by IPMA, Portugal's weather service, on Tuesday.

The month was also unusually hot. IPMA ranks it the **9th-warmest October since 2000** and the **5th-driest since 1931**.

## Rainfall Fell to Just 11 Percent of Normal Level

October's rainfall totalled only **12.3 millimetres** across mainland Portugal. That is far below what the month normally brings, based on the 1991-2020 reference period.

The shortfall was stark. The total rainfall came to **just 11%** of the normal amount for October.

## October Ranks as Fifth-Driest Since 1931

IPMA's records for mainland Portugal go back to **1931**. Against nearly a century of Octobers, this year's came in **5th-driest**.

By the end of the month, the dry spell had turned into something worse. **Meteorological drought worsened significantly**, IPMA said, hitting the Centre and South hardest.

The southern interior fared worst of all. Areas of **extreme drought** began appearing there for the first time.

## Average Temperature Climbed to 17.83C, Ninth-Warmest Since 2000

The average air temperature for October reached **17.83°C**. That is **1.04°C above** the normal value for the 1991-2020 reference period.

Despite the heat, this October did not break the record. The **warmest October on record** remains 2017, when the average hit 19.57°C.

Maximum daytime temperatures told a similar story. October ranked the **7th-highest** for maximum air temperature since 1931.

## Two Heat Waves Struck the Mainland Interior

IPMA recorded **two separate heat waves** during the month. The first ran from **10 to 17 October**, affecting the northern and central interior.

The second began on **28 October** and stretched into the first days of November. It covered the **Tagus Valley and Alentejo** region.

IPMA defines a heat wave as at least **six consecutive days** where the daily maximum temperature runs more than 5°C above the average daily maximum for that month, measured against the 1991-2020 reference period.

## Year's Record 10th Heat Wave Hit in Late October

That late-October heat wave was no isolated event. It was the **10th heat wave** of 2026, which IPMA says is a **record number** for a single year.

## Severe or Extreme Drought Now Covers 75 Percent of Mainland

By the end of October, drought conditions had spread across most of the mainland. IPMA classified **75% of the territory** as being in severe or extreme drought.

## Madeira Logged Its Eighth-Warmest October Since 1961

The picture was milder away from the mainland. In Madeira, October was the **8th-warmest since 1961**. In the Azores, IPMA described the month as **relatively warm**.
