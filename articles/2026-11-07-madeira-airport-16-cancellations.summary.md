# Madeira Airport Cancels 16 Flights as Storm Bruna Nears

Madeira International Airport had **16 cancelled flights** on Saturday morning, counting arrivals and departures. The cancellations came as the archipelago prepared for **Storm Bruna**.

The storm is expected to bring worse weather to Madeira **on Saturday and Sunday**. The airport operator warns that flights may be affected in the coming days.

## Nine Arrivals and Seven Departures Cancelled by Saturday Morning

The figures come from the website of **ANA Aeroportos**, the airport operator. At the time of the report, it listed **nine cancelled arrivals** and seven cancelled departures.

## Four Airlines Hit on Routes to Lisbon, Porto, Basel and Manchester

The affected routes link Madeira with Lisbon, Porto, Basel and Manchester. The flights were operated by **four airlines**.

They are **TAP Air Portugal**, easyJet Europe, easyJet Switzerland and Jet2.com.

## TAP Flight Due at 09:15 Diverted to Lisbon

Data from the tracking service **FlightRadar** showed more disruption on Saturday morning. A TAP flight from Lisbon was due to land in Madeira **at 09:15**.

It could **not land** at Madeira Airport. At the time of the report, it was being diverted back to Lisbon.

## Three Planes From Marseille, Brussels and Lisbon Circle Near the Airport

Three other aircraft were flying **holding patterns** near the airport. They were waiting for conditions that would allow them to land.

The planes had come from **Marseille, Brussels and Lisbon**, according to FlightRadar.

## Storm Bruna Forecast to Bring Gusts of up to 130 km/h

The disruption may be linked to **Storm Bruna** moving closer to the archipelago. The storm is expected to bring periods of rain, at times heavy, strong wind and conditions that can affect airport operations.

The Portuguese weather service, **IPMA**, forecasts persistent and sometimes heavy rain between Saturday and Sunday. Wind may reach **100 km/h** in low-lying areas and 130 km/h in the highlands.

Waves of **3.5 to 4.5 metres** are possible on Madeira's south coast. The regional authorities have also closed **[all classified hiking trails](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/hiking-trails-closed-sunday)** on Sunday, 8 November.

ANA has posted **a warning** on its website:

> "Weather forecasts point to adverse conditions that may affect operations (departures and arrivals) in the coming days. Contact your airline to confirm the status of your flight before going to the airport."

**ANA Aeroportos**, notice on its website

Passengers should follow **updates from their airlines** and check the information from ANA Aeroportos before they travel to the airport.
