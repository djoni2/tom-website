# IPMA Puts Madeira's South Coast and Peaks on Red Alert

Portugal's weather service, IPMA, has raised its rain warning to **red, the highest level**, for Madeira's south coast and mountainous areas. The alert takes effect from Tuesday night.

## Red Alert Runs Into Wednesday Morning

The red rain warning covers both areas from **21:00 on Tuesday**, 10 November, until 06:00 on Wednesday, 11 November. Forecasters expect rain that is at times strong and persistent.

## Wind Gusts Could Hit 140 Km/h

In the mountains, wind has also gone to red alert. Gusts there could reach **up to 140 km/h** between 00:00 and 06:00 on Wednesday.

Elsewhere, warnings stay lower. On the south coast, wind is under **orange alert** from 21:00 on Tuesday to midday on Wednesday, with gusts of up to 100 km/h.

The north coast and Porto Santo face similar orange and yellow alerts for rain and wind into Wednesday. Sea conditions also carry a yellow warning, with waves of **4 to 4.5 metres** expected off Madeira's coasts on Wednesday.

The Capitania do Porto do Funchal separately **updated its strong-sea warning** on Monday, forecasting waves of up to 4.5 metres on the north coast.
