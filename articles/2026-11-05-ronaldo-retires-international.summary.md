# Cristiano Ronaldo Retires From Portugal After 23 Years

**[Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)** has retired from international football. The 41-year-old announced the decision on social media on Thursday morning, 5 November.

He ends his career with Portugal after **23 years**. His caption read: "Portugal will always be in me."

The decision comes after a fall-out with head coach **[Jorge Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Jesus)** during the previous international break.

## A Record 146 Goals in 234 Games for Portugal

Sky Sports calls his tally **record-breaking**: 146 goals in 234 caps. He is first in the world for both goals and appearances in men's international football.

That is about **0.62 goals per game**, according to Sky Sports data.

## Euro 2016 and Two Nations League Titles Across 12 Major Tournaments

Ronaldo won **Euro 2016** with Portugal. He also won the Nations League **in 2019 and 2025**.

He made his debut **in August 2003**. He went on to play at six World Cups and **six European Championships**.

Portugal left the **most recent World Cup** in the round of 16. Spain, the eventual champions, beat them 1-0.

The Al Nassr forward had said the tournament would be **his last**. He later added that he would not make any rash decisions.

He also admitted in the last international break that he **had considered quitting** after the World Cup.

According to a statement on Instagram, he had **already written** a retirement statement. On the plane back from North America, federation president **[Pedro Proença](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Proen%C3%A7a)** talked him out of it.

## Ronaldo Hands Back the Number 7 Shirt in a Thursday Statement

The statement opens with a message to **the Portuguese people**. He wrote that "the day has come for me to hand back the number seven shirt to the national team."

He described a **decision of the heart**. "It was not an easy decision, because some decisions are not made with the mind alone," he wrote. "They are also made with the heart."

> "I spent my life working to become the best player in the world. Today, I realise my greatest victory was earning the honour of representing the best country in the world."

**Cristiano Ronaldo**, in his retirement statement

He thanked his **team-mates, coaches and staff**, his family and supporters around the world. "I had the privilege of being your captain, but no captain walks alone," he wrote.

He also spoke of **Portuguese people living abroad**. He wrote that, for ninety minutes, they "felt a little closer to home."

## In His Farewell, Ronaldo Recalls Running After a Football in Madeira

Near the end, Ronaldo returned to **his childhood on Madeira**. He thanked fans "for allowing me to live a dream that began when I was just a little boy in Madeira running after a football."

"The number 7 shirt shall remain. The pride of having worn it will be mine forever," he wrote. He ended with a **final goodbye**: "Farewell, until we meet again."

## Federation Plans a Tribute After His Final Game, a 1-0 Win Over Wales

The **Portuguese Football Federation** paid tribute to its former captain. It said a fitting tribute will follow **in the future**.

According to Sky Sports, his **last game for Portugal** was a 1-0 win against Wales.

> "More than just an outstanding athlete, our captain was an inspiring leader, an example of dedication, and the ultimate symbol of the spirit of our national team."

**Portuguese Football Federation**, in a statement

The federation also looked ahead to his **next goal target**: "Let's go for goal 1,000!"

## Four Players and Three Clubs Pay Tribute to Ronaldo

**Bruno Fernandes** said: "Thank you for the inspiration. It was a privilege to share great moments for our team with my childhood idol."

**Diogo Dalot** said: "You made us dream, you made us believe, you made us win. Thank you idol."

**Neymar** called him a "Legend."

**Pepe** said: "It was more than just football. Thank you for everything, Cris."

**Manchester United** said: "Congratulations on an incredible international career, Cristiano. Enjoy your retirement."

**Juventus** said: "Congratulations on an incredible journey with Portugal, Cristiano."

**Sporting CP** said: "Thank you for everything you did for our nation. Forever in the history of Portugal."

## Benched in a 2-1 Win Over Norway, He Left Camp Before Denmark

Jesus took over from **Roberto Martínez** after the World Cup. The new coach's first games were Nations League fixtures in **September and October**.

Ronaldo started the first game, the 1-0 win against Wales. He was then benched for a 2-1 win against Norway and **did not come on**.

On the eve of the game against Denmark on **1 October**, Ronaldo left the national team camp. Rumours followed that he was about to retire.

Jesus **denied any rift** between the two. But Ronaldo said in a statement on Instagram that the coach had broken promises.

According to Ronaldo, Jesus had told him on the day of the game that he would play **30 minutes** against Norway.

Ronaldo **described the match**: "Everyone knows the rest. I warmed up from half-time onwards, watched the minutes tick by and wasn't called on," he said. "The coach had broken his word to me."

## An Eight-Point Document and a Second Broken Promise, Ronaldo Says

Ronaldo and Jesus, who once worked together **at Al Nassr**, met after the Norway game. Al Nassr sporting director **Simão Coutinho** was also there.

According to Ronaldo's statement, Jesus agreed to say that his captain **never demanded minutes**. Jesus would also say it was a mistake to let him warm up without bringing him on.

Ronaldo says an **eight-point document** summed up a later meeting. It involved Jesus, Coutinho and Tiago Craveiro, the player's strategic adviser.

Ronaldo felt the coach's **next press conference** did not match their talk. He claims the talk ended with Jesus asking him for a hug.

"It was the second time he had broken his word to me. I decided to leave the training camp without waiting for any further broken promises," Ronaldo said. Jesus later **denied any such agreement** at a press conference.

## Ronaldo Received a 30-Day Ban and a £215 Fine

Ronaldo still admitted he **should never have left** the camp, "despite the absolute breach of trust" from Jesus.

He **apologised** to his team-mates and the Portuguese people. He said he would accept any punishment from the federation.

The federation then gave him a **30-day ban** and a £215 fine. Times of Madeira [reported on the ban](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/2026-10-19-witzstrafe-fuere-cristiano-ronaldo-nach-portugal-zoff-sport) in October.
