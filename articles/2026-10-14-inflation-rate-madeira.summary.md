# Madeira Inflation Rises to 3.8% in September

The inflation rate in Madeira **is** 3.8% in September 2026, up 0.1 percentage points from August. This shows the second consecutive monthly increase, according to data released by the Regional Directorate of Statistics (DREM).

Goods prices rose 2.6% while services increased by 5.2%. The largest increases were seen in **restaurants and accommodation services** (7.6%), transport (6.8%), and housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels (4.1%). The only sector showing negative variation was information and communication at -1.7%.

> "The acceleration of CPI (Consumer Price Index) is largely explained by the increase in fuel prices"

**National Institute of Statistics (INE)**

:::callout
**What this is**: The Consumer Price Index measures price changes. The INE is Portugal's official statistics agency.
:::

Year-on-year, inflation in Madeira was 4.6% in September 2026 compared to 3.6% nationally. National inflation **also accelerated** to 3.6% in September from 3.3% in August, according to the INE. These figures show Madeira's inflation remains above the national average.

The data suggests inflationary trends may continue without targeted intervention in key sectors like **energy and housing**. Policymakers face challenges in balancing economic growth with price stability measures. Recent data indicates that without effective **monetary policy** measures, inflation could remain elevated for an extended period, affecting household budgets across the region.
