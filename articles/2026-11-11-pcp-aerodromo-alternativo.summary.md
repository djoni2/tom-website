# PCP Proposes Backup Airport Study and Cuts to Madeira Fees

The Portuguese Communist Party said on Wednesday that it had filed **two amendments** to Portugal's 2027 State Budget. Both were submitted to the Assembleia da República, the national parliament, and both target air transport in Madeira and Porto Santo.

## Backup Airport Study Sought for 2027

The first amendment asks the central government to fund **preliminary studies** during 2027. The goal is to test technical solutions for building an aerodrome that would complement [Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/albuquerque-speaks-of-second-airport-for-madeira), the region's only airport.

## Single Airport Called a Safety Risk

PCP says having **no backup airport** is a weakness for security and civil protection. It blocks the use of aircraft that fight fires, the party says. It also leaves the island with no alternative when the main airport closes because of bad weather.

## Fees Called 78% Higher Than Azores

The second amendment calls on the government to press ANA - Aeroportos de Portugal on airport fees. It wants **yearly increases stopped** and an effective cut secured for passengers at the Madeira and Porto Santo airports.

PCP says that in 2025 and 2026, passenger service fees at Madeira's airports were higher than Lisbon's, and **about 78% higher** than in the Azores. The party says this penalizes residents, passengers and the regional economy.

PCP describes the situation as **unacceptable** and is demanding concrete answers from the central government.
