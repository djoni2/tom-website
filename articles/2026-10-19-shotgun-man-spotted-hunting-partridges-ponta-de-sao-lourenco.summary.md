# Shotgun Man Spotted Hunting Partridges at Ponta de São Lourenço

By **Mat**

## Incident Details

A **shotgun man** was spotted hunting partridges at Ponta de São Lourenço, violating the hunting ban in this protected area. The incident occurred on Sunday, October 18, 2026, along the popular walking path used by tourists and locals.

## Safety Concerns

According to what we were told by a witness, the individual would be hunting partridges and had fired in an area where tourists were walking. **Hunting is prohibited** at Ponta de São Lourenço due to constant human presence. Tourists and locals frequently use this scenic coastal trail for recreation and enjoy its natural beauty.

## Ecological Context

The red partridge (Alectoris rufa) is frequently sighted at Ponta de São Lourenço, and this area is protected because of its ecological value. **Public safety** is at risk when hunting occurs in this popular area. Both tourists and madeirenses value this trail for recreation and relaxation.
