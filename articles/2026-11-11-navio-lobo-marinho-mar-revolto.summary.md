# Ferry Lobo Marinho Battles Rough Seas Off Santa Cruz

A video filmed off the coast of **Santa Cruz** on Wednesday shows the ferry Lobo Marinho fighting rough seas as **storm Charles** battered Madeira. The footage captures the force of the waves hitting the vessel.

## Ferry Left Funchal Port at 07:00 Wednesday

The ship left the **Port of Funchal** at around 07:00 that morning. The operation aimed to move it away from the **Pontinha** area, which had been badly hit by rough seas.

## Porto Santo Line Check-In Area Suffers Wave Damage

The bad weather also caused serious damage to the **check-in area** of Porto Santo Line. Waves broke over the curtain wall there.

## Ferry Sheltered Between Porto da Cruz and Santana

After leaving Funchal, the ferry sought shelter along the island's **north coast**, between Porto da Cruz and Santana. It waited there for the sea conditions to improve.

Only the crew was on board, under the command of **Maurílio Mendes**, a Madeiran captain. No passengers were aboard during the operation.

The ship's passage past Santa Cruz was filmed by **Marco Jarimba**, of the channel Madeira Runway Spotting. The footage captured the waves and the difficult conditions the vessel faced.
