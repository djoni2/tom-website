# Albuquerque Says "I Build Houses" Amid Housing Protest

Miguel Albuquerque, the president of Madeira's Regional Government, brushed off a **housing rights protest** planned for Funchal this Saturday. Asked about the demonstration, he pointed to his government's own construction record instead.

> I build houses and then they go protest.

**Miguel Albuquerque**, President of the Regional Government

He made the remark on the sidelines of an event in **Porto Santo**, when reporters asked him about the planned march.

## Housing March Takes to Funchal's Streets This Saturday

Albuquerque said he has "nothing against the protests." But he argued the **real fix for housing** is building more homes, not marching for them.

The march is organized by the Terreiro das Lutas group and the Plataforma Casas para Viver Madeira. It joins a **national day of mobilization** for housing rights.

## Program Already Delivers Nearly 800 Public Homes

Albuquerque said his Executive has delivered **around 800 homes** through its public housing program so far. He pointed to this record as the government's answer to the housing crisis.

## Waiting List Still Holds 5,000 to 6,000 Families

Even with that program, the backlog remains large. Regional estimates put the number of families on waiting lists for **social housing alone** at 5,000 to 6,000. That figure excludes middle-income families priced out of the private rental market.

Those 800 homes cover about **13 percent** of that formal backlog, using the upper end of the estimate.

## Investment in Public Housing Tops 175 Million Euros

The public construction program now represents an investment of **175 million euros**, according to the figures Albuquerque presented.

## Albuquerque Says He Saw the Crisis Coming Three Years Ago

"And I got ahead of the country," he said. He explained that **about three years ago**, he had already identified the risk. Economic growth, housing demand, foreign investment and growing short-term rentals were squeezing access to housing, he said.

That assessment, he said, led the Executive to launch its public construction program and open **several tenders** for new housing developments.

> We will continue to build quality public housing for families.

**Miguel Albuquerque**, President of the Regional Government
