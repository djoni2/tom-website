# Madeira Car Rental Contracts Rise 7.1% as Revenue Falls

Car rental companies in Madeira signed **136,764 contracts** for vehicles without a driver in the third quarter of 2026. That is 7.1% more than in the same period of 2025, according to the Regional Statistics Office of Madeira (DREM).

Despite the extra bookings, the firms' combined **revenue fell 2.6%** to 39.3 million euros. The figures come from DREM's Quarterly Survey of Vehicle Rental Companies (ITEAVRAM). It covers cars, motorcycles and motorhomes rented without a driver.

From January to September 2026, the industry recorded **355,108 contracts**, up 3.5% on the same nine months of 2025. Revenue over that longer period reached 90.9 million euros, an increase of 2.9%.

## Airport Pickups Reach 59.5% Of All Rental Contracts

The airport remained the **top pickup point** for rental vehicles. It accounted for 59.5% of all contracts in the quarter, up 10.6% year-on-year.

Rental company premises came second, with **30.7% of contracts**, down 1.6%. Hotels made up the remaining 9.8%, up 16.6%, the fastest-growing of the three channels.

## Average Car Rental Now Lasts 7.8 Days

Drivers kept their light passenger cars for longer. The average rental ran **7.8 days** in the third quarter, up 20.8% from a year earlier.

Motorcycles followed the opposite trend. Their average rental **fell to 3.0 days**, down 19.9%. Motorhomes stayed closer to cars, averaging 7.0 days, up 2.2%.

## Fleet Shrinks To 13,205 Vehicles As Electrics Surge

The total rental fleet **shrank to 13,205 vehicles** by the end of the quarter, a drop of 2.3% from a year earlier. Light passenger cars made up 97.7% of that fleet, with motorcycles at 2.2% and motorhomes at 0.1%.

The mix of fuel types shifted further away from fossil fuels. Petrol vehicles still dominated at **78.9% of the fleet**, but that share fell 2.7%. Diesel fell harder, down 19.2% to 10.6% of the fleet.

Hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles grew 20.3% to 7.6% of the fleet. **Electric vehicles jumped 56.6%** to 2.9% of the total.

## Germany Edges Out Portugal As Top Rental Market

**Germany led all markets**, supplying 19.5% of rental clients in the quarter. Portugal followed closely at 19.4%, with France in third place at 12.2%.

The United Kingdom supplied **8.7% of clients**, ahead of Poland at 7.8%, Spain at 6.4% and the Netherlands at 5.8%. Together, these seven markets accounted for 79.7% of all clients, the same share they held over the first nine months of the year.

## Revenue Falls To 39.3 Million Euros Despite More Bookings

Total revenue dropped to **39.3 million euros**, 2.6% less than the same quarter of 2025. The base rental service made up most of that, at 31.2 million euros, down 6.9%.

Other revenue, which includes extras such as insurance and equipment, rose **19.0% to 8.1 million** euros. DREM does not break down what those other charges cover.
