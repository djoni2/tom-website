# Cost of Living Protests Reach Funchal as Madeira Inflation Rises

## Protests Spread Nationwide

**STFPS Madeira** has called a manifestation "Contra o Empobrecimento, pelos Direitos e por uma Vida Melhor" for Saturday at 10:00 in Funchal. This joins similar protests against cost of living increases happening in Porto, Coimbra and Lisbon.

**Madeira inflation** remains above the national average, further increasing the cost of essential goods and services. Given this reality, it falls to the Regional Government to adopt concrete measures responding to workers' increased difficulties. The STFPS union notes that housing, fuel and food prices have risen sharply.

**Movement demands** include six main points. These are: below rental law, end evictions, stop property purchases by vulture funds, more public housing, recover vacant properties for housing, and implement rental caps. These demands show widespread concerns about housing speculation and affordability.

**Recent events in Porto** show protest intensity. Police removed tents from demonstrators, leading to rough sleeping.

**Police charge** injured 39 participants with two in serious condition. Despite this, thousands later joined the protests, showing growing public concern about living costs.
