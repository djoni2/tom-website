Between 1935 and 1939, Portuguese media documented the arrival of thousands of German KdF visitors with notable attention. /quote It is pleasing for us to record that Portugal is the first country to receive this embassy of peace and work /quote and observers noted their conduct was "exemplary 'order, discipline and joy'". They were described as "fat ladies, strong men, non-idle workers" who visited cities "in dense groups" and frequented "cafés and breweries."

Since "Germany is the country that annually sends highest number of tourists to Portugal," the KdF program was considered "our best propaganda agent," with some dedicating themselves "more to the pleasure of travel" through these visits.

Returning workers recalled their experiences with fondness, describing their time in Portugal as "full of warmth of the southern climate" and feeling /quote vibrant with welcome of our people and impregnated with the balm of a 15-day journey in the blue sphere of the Atlantic /quote.
