# Madeira Under Yellow Rain Warning on Thursday

Portugal's weather service, IPMA, has put Madeira's south coast and mountain areas under a **yellow warning** for rain this Thursday, 5 November.

Forecasters expect showers that will **at times be heavy**. They may come with thunderstorms.

## When the Warning Applies

In both areas, the warning runs from **09:00 to 15:00** UTC on Thursday.

It then **starts again at midnight** and runs until 15:00 on Friday, 6 November.

## Worse Weather Expected at the Weekend

The weather is expected to **get worse** over the weekend.

In recent days, the authorities have issued **several recommendations** for the public to follow in bad weather.
