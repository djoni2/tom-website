# IPMA Raises Madeira Rain Warning to Orange for Sunday Morning

Portugal's weather service has raised its rain warning for parts of Madeira to **orange**. The Portuguese Institute for Sea and Atmosphere (IPMA) expects showers that will at times be heavy and come with thunderstorms.

The orange warning covers the **South Coast** and the **mountain regions** of Madeira island. These are the areas where the weather is expected to be at its worst.

## Orange Warning Runs From 00:00 to 09:00 on Sunday, 1 November

The orange warning will be in force from **midnight until 09:00** on Sunday, 1 November. IPMA expects conditions to get worse during this period.

The same areas will first be under a **yellow warning** from 21:00 on Saturday until midnight. After 09:00 on Sunday, the alert drops back to yellow and stays in place until 12:00.

## Civil Protection Urges Residents to Step Up Preventive Measures

The Regional Civil Protection Service is warning residents that the **weather will worsen** in Madeira over the coming days. It is asking people to strengthen their preventive measures, as there may be periods of heavy rain.

## North Coast and Porto Santo Also Placed Under Yellow Warnings

The bad weather is not limited to the south. The **North Coast** of Madeira is also under a yellow rain warning, from 21:00 on Saturday until 12:00 on Sunday. Heavy showers and thunderstorms are possible there too.

On **Porto Santo**, IPMA has issued a yellow warning for Sunday, from 06:00 to 15:00, for the same reason.

As a result, the **whole region** will be under weather warnings for part of Sunday morning. The highest risk remains on Madeira's South Coast and high ground, between 00:00 and 09:00.
