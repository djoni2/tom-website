# PSD Leader Says Housing Protesters Live in Luxury Homes

Jaime Filipe Ramos, the parliamentary group leader of the Social Democratic Party (PSD) in Madeira's Legislative Assembly, said on Tuesday that he respects housing protesters. But he added that he has seen many people at housing demonstrations who **live in luxury homes** themselves.

According to Ramos, those people, not the protesters, are the ones who **disrespect the housing movement**. He made the comment at the end of a debate on a resolution proposed by the JPP, the Juntos pelo Povo party.

## JPP Resolution Cites 2023 Affordable Housing Decree

The JPP motion asked the Regional Government to **regulate a 2023 decree** on affordable housing. That decree, Regional Legislative Decree No. 25/2023/M, created an incentive programme called "Casa Própria" (Own Home) to promote affordable housing.

The Legislative Assembly held a **general debate** on the resolution before Ramos made his closing remarks.

## Rubina Leal Rejects Study Request in Funchal Session

During the same session, the JPP also asked **Rubina Leal**, the president of the Legislative Assembly, to hand over a government study on housing. Leal said she could not accept the request because she **does not have** the study.
