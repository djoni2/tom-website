# Tourists Hang Hammocks From Road Signs at Madeira Airport

Two tourists set up a **makeshift camp** on a pedestrian path at Madeira Airport, near the entrance to the terminal. They used road signs to hang two hammocks, the newspaper JM reported.

A reader sent JM a **photo of the scene**. JM described the situation as unusual and said it raises questions about the behaviour of some visitors in public spaces.

## Hammocks Tied to Road Signs on the Morning of 4 November

The episode took place **on Wednesday morning**, 4 November, at the airport, one of the main gateways to Madeira. According to reports, it lasted for some time, JM wrote.

The two tourists, who JM said **appeared to be foreign**, turned an area meant for pedestrians into a place to rest.

They used the **vertical road signs** to put up the two hammocks, creating a kind of open-air "tent". Their bags were placed next to them.

## Two Visitors Watched by Passing Tourists and Drivers at the Terminal

The scene **did not go unnoticed**. Other tourists walking nearby watched with smiles, as did several drivers who passed by, according to JM.

The newspaper did not report **any intervention** by the airport, the police or other authorities.

## JM Says Similar Images From Across Madeira Upset Residents

According to JM, the photo is **one more image** sent by a reader. It joins many others from across the island that are starting to cause anger among residents.

The newspaper wrote that the episode shows **abusive behaviour** by some visitors, who "seem to confuse hospitality with an absence of rules".

**Times of Madeira** has reported earlier on the regional government's view that [illegal camping is a municipal issue](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/illegal-camping-is-a-municipal-issue-says-government).
