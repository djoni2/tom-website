# Madeira Births Fall Faster Than Deaths in August

## Overview of August 2026 Statistics

Madeira recorded **238 deaths** and **136 births** in August 2026. This resulted in a natural population decrease of 102 people. While mortality decreased by 1.7% compared to August 2025, births fell by 11.7% over the same period.

## Birth-Death Gap Analysis

The gap between births and deaths widened despite the decline in mortality. This produced a natural balance of **-102 individuals**, which is less favorable than the **-88 recorded** in August 2025. For the first eight months of 2026, however, the natural balance improved from -862 to -766 people compared to the same period last year.

## Detailed Demographics

Between January and August, Madeira recorded **1,926 deaths** (28 fewer than in 2025, a 1.4% reduction). During the same period, there were **1,160 births** (68 more than in 2025, a 6.2% increase). August deaths were 11.5% above the 2016-2019 average of 214 deaths per month.

In August, **113 men** (47.5%) and **125 women** (52.5%) died. Among those aged 65 and over, 21.8% were aged 65-74, 35.7% were 75-84, and 31.9% were 85 or older. No infant or fetal deaths were recorded.

More boys than girls were born: **71 male** and **65 female** infants. Marriages increased in August 2026, with 129 ceremonies (10.3% more than August 2025). However, the January-August total of 761 marriages was 3.5% below the 2025 figure.

:::callout
**Context**: These statistics come from preliminary data released by Portugal's National Statistics Institute (INE) and regionalized by Madeira's Regional Directorate of Statistics (DREM). The data shows trends that may influence future policy decisions on healthcare, family support, and social services.
:::
