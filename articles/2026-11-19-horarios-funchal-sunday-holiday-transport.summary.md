# Horários do Funchal Denies Cutting Sunday Bus Trips

Horários do Funchal, the company that runs Funchal's bus network, said on Thursday that it is **not cutting** any Sunday or holiday services. The company said its current schedules and routes stay in place on those days, with no trip reductions planned.

The statement, sent to Diário de Notícias, responded to a report the newspaper had published two days earlier. That report, based on a statement by CDU leader Duarte Martins, said the company had **slashed Sunday bus trips**.

Horários do Funchal said the gap between weekday and Sunday trip numbers **follows the usual schedule**. It did not announce any change to the service already in place.

## Sunday Timetable Lists 1,332 Trips Versus 2,441 on Weekdays

The company pointed to an analysis by the **'PAROU' platform**, the same data source cited in Tuesday's report. It found **2,441 scheduled trips** on a normal weekday across Funchal's network, against **1,332** on Sundays.

Horários do Funchal attributed the lower Sunday figure to **reduced demand**. Schools, public services and many workplaces close on Sundays, the company said. It added that routes with no Sunday trips mainly serve **schools** in the municipality.

The company also said the service it runs is set by its **concession contract** with the Regional Government. That contract follows a plan approved by the Institute of Mobility and Transport. Any change to it would need to go through the concession's own approval process.

## Funchal Ranks Fifth for Sunday Coverage Among 16 Networks

Horários do Funchal cited the same PAROU analysis to defend its Sunday service level. Funchal placed **fifth** for Sunday coverage among the **16 bus networks** the platform studied, the company said.

## 97% of Weekday Stops Keep Service Within 100 Meters

The company said **97%** of stops served on a weekday also have Sunday service. Riders can use the same stop or find an alternative **within 100 meters**, it said.

## CDU's Tuesday Report Put the Cut at 45%

Two days earlier, CDU leader Duarte Martins said Horários do Funchal had cut **45%** of its Sunday trips. He cited the same 1,109-trip gap between weekdays and Sundays and said **eight routes** had been eliminated entirely.

CDU also said almost **three in ten stops** lose their last bus of the day before 21:00. The party called the cuts unfair to shift workers, young people, families and elderly residents without another way to travel.

Horários do Funchal's new statement **did not directly address** the 45% figure or CDU's count of eliminated routes. It said only that the overall Sunday and holiday service remains unchanged and follows the approved concession plan.

The two sides are not disputing the trip numbers. They disagree on what those numbers mean. CDU's **45% figure** compares today's Sunday timetable with today's weekday timetable. It is not a measured drop from an earlier Sunday schedule.

Neither CDU nor Horários do Funchal published a past timetable showing what Sunday service looked like before. CDU's statement does not say when any reduction took place, so its use of the word "cut" describes the **size of the gap**, not a confirmed change.

Horários do Funchal's position is that there was no change to confirm. It says weekday and Sunday schedules have always differed by close to that margin. Without older data from either side, neither **claim can be checked**.
