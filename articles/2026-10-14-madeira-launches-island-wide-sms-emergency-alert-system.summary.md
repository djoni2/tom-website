# Madeira Launches Island-Wide SMS Emergency Alert System

Madeira will send **emergency alerts** to residents and visitors via SMS during risk or emergency situations. The system covers everyone on the island regardless of nationality or mobile service provider.

Messages will be sent in **Portuguese or English** based on technical criteria to identify recipients. 

The alert system operates through **protocols signed between** Madeira's Regional Civil Protection Service and telecom operators MEO, NOS, and Vodafone.

> "a determining step in implementing the new regional risk monitoring and communication system, special alert, and population warning"

**Micaela Freitas**, Regional Secretary of Health and Civil Protection

:::callout
**What this is**: Madeira's Regional Civil Protection Service coordinates disaster response and emergency management across the Portuguese archipelago, working with national and international agencies to protect residents and visitors.
:::

The mechanism aims to strengthen **speed, reach, and effectiveness** of information transmitted to the population in dangerous situations. It enables citizens to receive guidance and adopt self-protection behaviors.

Signing of the agreements this morning integrates the **Disaster Risk Reduction week** program. 

The **operational date** for the system has not been disclosed. 

Officials say the **SMS alerts will complement** existing siren and media-based warning systems already in place across Madeira's municipalities. 

The **system represents** a significant upgrade to Madeira's emergency notification capabilities. 

It will particularly help reach **tourists and visitors** who may not have local phone numbers or be familiar with traditional alert methods.
