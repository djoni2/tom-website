# Madeira MP Demands Answers on Lufthansa's TAP Stake

A Madeira MP wants the national government to explain what the **TAP sale**
means for people on the islands. Lufthansa is set to hold **49.9% of TAP**.

Emanuel Câmara, of PS-Madeira, sits in the Assembleia da República, the
national parliament. He is sending a **request for clarification** to Miguel
Pinto Luz, the Minister of Infrastructure and Housing.

Câmara wants to know how the interests of people in Madeira and Porto Santo
were protected in the talks that led to the privatisation. Air transport has a
**determining role** in island mobility, he says, because the region is a group
of islands.

> "This is a matter the Government cannot avoid. The Portuguese who live on the islands need answers."

**Emanuel Câmara**, PS-Madeira MP in the Assembleia da República

## The Wider Complaint

For Câmara, the sale fits a pattern. He says the **AD government** has
mistreated and disrespected people in Madeira and Porto Santo over territorial
continuity. He points to continuing problems with the **Territorial Continuity
Mechanism**, the former Social Mobility Subsidy, which he says still harms
residents of the region.

The report does **not say** when the minister has to answer, and it carries no
reply from the government.
