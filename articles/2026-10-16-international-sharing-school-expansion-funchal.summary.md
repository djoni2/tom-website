# International Sharing School Invests €20M in Funchal Expansion

The International Sharing School in Madeira has invested **over 20 million euros** in new installations in central Funchal. This investment will **double the school's capacity** from 240 to 500 students through phased growth.

Francisco Ladeira Santos, CEO of Sharing Education Group, called it the **largest private education investment** in the Madeira region. The school recovered a historic building without waiting for financial aid, focusing on quality facilities for current students.

Tuition ranges from **600 to 1,400 euros**, with agreements in place with professional organizations and municipalities including Funchal to improve accessibility for middle-class families. The school also receives annual subsidies allocated to private education institutions in the region.

Currently, about **50% Portuguese** and the rest are international. The school aims to maintain and grow its Madeiran community alongside its international student body.

> "This school has a very large Madeiran component that we want to continue and we want to continue growing with Madeirans too, not only with foreigners"

**Francisco Ladeira Santos**, CEO of Sharing Education Group

:::callout
**About the school**: International Sharing School Madeira offers international education from early years through secondary level, following international curricula while maintaining a strong connection to the local Madeiran community.
:::

The historic hall being renovated will become a **music room, multimedia lab**. Completion is expected by the end of the academic year, with opening scheduled for September 2027.
