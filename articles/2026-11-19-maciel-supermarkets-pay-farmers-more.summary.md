# Maciel Urges Supermarkets to Pay Madeira's Farmers More

Large supermarket chains should **pay Madeiran farmers more**, in line with rising shelf prices. That appeal came today from Nuno Maciel, the regional secretary for Agriculture and Fisheries.

Maciel spoke on the sidelines of the 8th Annual Meeting of the Continente Producers' Club, held at the **Museu da Banana** in Lugar de Baixo.

## Secretary Speaks at 8th Producers' Club Meeting at Museu da Banana

> If the final price has risen significantly, it is also important that there is recognition for our farmers and their production.

**Nuno Maciel**, Regional Secretary for Agriculture and Fisheries

Maciel pointed to **rising costs of fertilizers** and fuel as pressure factors on farming. They warned that without better pay for producers, some crops could stop being grown at all.

> If we do not raise the price paid to our producers, it is obvious that there are times when that production stops being economically viable. And if it stops being economically viable, we will have to import.

**Nuno Maciel**, Regional Secretary for Agriculture and Fisheries

## Vegetables Account for €40 Million of €157 Million in Sales

According to the **latest official data**, from 2024, vegetables made up about €40 million of all first sales in the region's primary sector. Total sales that year reached €157 million.

## Local Produce Reaches Tables in Hours, Imports Take Six Days

Maciel highlighted the value of **local production** for freshness and quality. Vegetables picked in the morning in Madeira can reach a consumer's table by lunchtime, they said.

Imported produce, by contrast, can take **at least six days** to reach supermarket shelves.

## Marca Madeira Brand Covers 4,402 Registered Producers

Maciel also called for a **stronger push** behind Marca Madeira, the regional label for local produce. The brand currently covers **4,402 producers** who are supported by regional services.
