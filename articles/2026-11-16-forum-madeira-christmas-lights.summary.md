# Forum Madeira Lights Up For Christmas On November 20

Forum Madeira will switch on its Christmas lights at **19:00 on November 20**. The ceremony takes place in the shopping centre's Praça Central. It features a performance by the children's choir of the Conservatório - Escola das Artes da Madeira.

## Santa Claus Arrives With Snowfall At Noon

The programme continues the next day. At **11:00 on November 21**, the Teatro do Bolo do Caco performs the 'Pasteleiros Mágicos' show. At noon, Santa Claus arrives at the shopping centre, with his descent accompanied by **falling snow**.

## Santa's House Welcomes Visits Until December 24

Santa's House, known locally as Casa do Pai Natal, will receive visitors for photographs **until December 24**. Families can stop by for a picture with Santa through Christmas Eve.

## Free Christmas Train Runs Until The Last Day Of December

A Christmas Train will operate inside the centre until the last day of the year. Participation is **free**, but visitors must **register through the app**.

## Christmas Market Opens At Praça Central On November 26

Starting **November 26**, the Praça Central hosts a Christmas market featuring local brands and projects. Among the new additions is CêCafé, a social project run by Casa São José. The project focuses on training and professional integration for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

## Floor 3 Gardens Host Free Shows On Three December Dates

The gardens on the shopping centre's third floor will host a creative workshop on **December 1**. The Teatro do Bolo do Caco also returns there for free shows on December **8, 19 and 23**, each starting at 11:00.
