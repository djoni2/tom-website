# Machico Suspends Zoning Plan to Clear Way for New Hotels

Machico's municipal council has **unanimously approved** a partial suspension of its master plan. The move is meant to unblock two large-scale hotel investments, broadcaster RTP Madeira reported on 12 November.

The suspension affects the **Plano Diretor Municipal**, the territorial plan known as the PDM that normally governs what can be built where. Councillors from every party backed the change, according to the report.

## Council Approves Suspension by Unanimous Vote

All of Machico's **political forces** supported the decision, RTP Madeira said. The council argued that two specific zones should not have to wait for the planning instrument to be revised before hotel investment could move in.

> We suspended the PDM exactly because we believe these two zones should not have to wait for a revision of the plan for this investment to happen.

**Machico's Municipal Council**

## Dom Pedro Site Covers 6,000 Square Metres

One of the two plots sits next to **Dom Pedro**, covering about 6,000 square metres. The report did not give further detail on what will be built there or by whom.

## Misericórdia Hillside Plot Spans Over 7 Hectares

The second, much larger plot lies on the **Misericórdia hillside** and spans more than 7 hectares. Together, the two sites are the target of the council's partial suspension.

## Council Sets 24-Month Horizon for Ground Work

The council expects **licensing procedures** to move forward as soon as next year. It is working toward a horizon of up to 24 months before construction becomes visible on either site.

> We are working here with a horizon of up to a maximum of two years to actually see something emerge on the ground, which will be the first consequence of this suspension of the PDM.

**Machico's Municipal Council**

## PSD Backs Plan but Wants Broader Housing Suspension

The opposition **PSD voted in favour**, but argued the council should have gone further. It wanted the suspension to also cover land earmarked for housing.

> We believe this suspension is, in fact, important for Machico's development, but it should have been broader.

**PSD, Machico's Opposition Party**

PSD said it had recommended **extending the measure** to collective housing projects, not just hotels. It pointed to delays in the wider PDM revision as a brake on the municipality's development. The party argued Machico has a pressing need for housing, whether offered at reduced cost or not.

For now, the suspension stays limited to the **two hotel sites**. The required procedures must still run their course before the measure takes legal effect.
