# Over Half of Portuguese Consumers Lost Purchasing Power in Last Year

**53% of Portuguese consumers** report decreased purchasing power last year, per Deco, Universidade Católica and Mercadona study.

This reveals the **widespread impact** of rising living costs across Portugal.

**Only 13%** of consumers saw any increase in purchasing power, with just 4% reporting a significant increase and 9% a slight increase. The majority perception remains negative, highlighting the ongoing economic pressure on households.

:::callout
**Context**: The study surveyed 2,000 adults across all 18 districts of mainland Portugal, providing a nationally representative view of consumer perceptions and behaviours regarding purchasing power and cost of living.
:::

Consumers are adapting their behaviours in response to these pressures, with **78% indicating** they have altered their purchasing habits in some way. Strategies include increased price comparison, promotion hunting, and choosing own-brand products - tactics used by approximately 75% of consumers.

Despite the growth of online shopping, **physical stores** remain the dominant channel for grocery shopping.

With **over 90%** of consumers doing most of their food purchases in supermarkets and hypermarkets.

Loyalty programmes are **widely used**, with 83% of consumers utilising them occasionally and 87% finding them at least moderately advantageous.
