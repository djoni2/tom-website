# Man Found Dead on Praia Formosa Beach in Funchal

A man was found dead on **Praia Formosa beach** in Funchal on Thursday morning. The victim has been identified as a resident of Câmara de Lobos. Authorities responded immediately and have begun investigating the circumstances surrounding the death.

The Polícia Judiciária is **leading the investigation**. They are working to determine what happened and whether the death is connected to any criminal activity. José Matos, coordinator of the Judicial Police in Madeira, confirmed that investigators are collecting evidence to reconstruct the events.

While police have **not ruled out** the possibility of foul play, they emphasize that the investigation is ongoing. No definitive conclusions have been reached.

Officials are urging anyone with information to come forward. The **investigation includes** forensic examination of the scene and interviews with witnesses. Authorities are also reviewing surveillance footage from nearby establishments.

They are **checking bank transactions** and mobile phone records as part of standard procedures. They are conducting a thorough and careful examination.

:::callout
**Praia Formosa context**: This is Funchal's main public beach, popular with locals and tourists.
:::
