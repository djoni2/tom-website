# Madeira Taxis Call for One-Vehicle Cap on TVDE Licences

The **Taxis-RAM Association** wants Madeira's regional government to limit TVDE operation to one vehicle per licence. It wants the cap applied to all licences already in use, RTP Madeira reported on 18 November.

TVDE is the Portuguese term for **paid passenger transport** in unmarked private cars, the sector in which ride-hailing apps compete with taxis.

## Cap Would Apply to About 500 Existing Licences

Paulo Pereira, the **president of Taxis-RAM**, said there are around 500 TVDE licences in Madeira. If each licence were limited to one vehicle, he said, that would mean about **500 cars** in total.

He is asking the regional government to take the demand into account. It is now drafting a **new regional decree** for the TVDE sector.

## President Cites Market Study Presented on 4 November

Pereira said he wants to draw the attention of **transport authorities** to the relationship between the TVDE and taxi sectors. He pointed to a regional study on TVDE that, he said, already found the market to be saturated.

> This call for attention to the transport authorities is about there being a service provided between these two sectors, TVDE and taxis, and not, as the regional TVDE study itself already showed, a market that is completely saturated. So knowing the market is saturated, it makes no sense to have more than one vehicle per existing licence.

**Paulo Pereira**, President of Taxis-RAM

Times of Madeira previously reported that the **regional secretary** for Equipment and Infrastructure presented the conclusions of that study to transport sector representatives on 4 November.

## Taxis-RAM Says 273 Taxis Are Ready for TVDE

While pushing for the cap, Taxis-RAM also revealed it is preparing its own fleet to enter the TVDE market. The association says it already has **273 vehicles** with applications filed to operate TVDE services.

> We already have written applications ready for 273 taxis to enter. Right now we have 30 vehicles already working in the premium category, the letter-A and letter-T taxis. On top of those 20 or 30, we have 273 vehicles registered and ready to enter, once the law allows it here in the autonomous region of Madeira.

**Paulo Pereira**, President of Taxis-RAM

Pereira gave the current premium fleet as "30" and then, in the same answer, as "20 or 30". The **source transcript** does not settle which figure is correct.

Those taxis would work on the **same platform** as TVDE cars, he said, charging the fares set out in Law No. 59 of 2026.

## Government Extended the Licence Freeze Last Week

Pereira's comments come a week after the regional government extended its suspension of **new TVDE licences**. The freeze was pushed back by four months.

[Times of Madeira reported](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/madeira-extends-tvde-licence-freeze-by-four-months) that the Council of the Government approved the extension on 12 November. It keeps a freeze first ordered in **September 2025** in place, while the licensed TVDE fleet has grown to about 800 vehicles.
