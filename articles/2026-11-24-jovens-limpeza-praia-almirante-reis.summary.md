# Young Volunteers Collect 53 Kilos of Trash in Funchal

More than **20 young people** from Madeira and Austria collected 53 kilos of trash on Tuesday. They worked at Praia do Almirante Reis and a garden next to the Funchal Cable Car, as part of the European Week for **Waste Prevention**.

The association **My Madeira Island** organized the event with the Austrian group Green Heroes Austria. It brought together students from Vienna and Funchal for an awareness campaign on **responsible production and consumption**.

## Breakdown Shows Eight Kilos of Plastic and Almost 1,000 Cigarette Butts

Organizers sorted the waste by type after the clean-up. Volunteers found **eight kilos of plastic**, three kilos of glass, five kilos of undifferentiated trash and about 1,000 cigarette butts.

## Storm-Dragged Metal Bars Piled Up Near Funchal's Cable Car

Most of the total weight, however, came from **metal bars**. Organizers believe recent storms swept them onto the beach. The clean-up also covered the garden beside the **Funchal Cable Car** station.

> Young people from Vienna and Funchal live very different realities, but find the same objects on the ground.

**Anastasia Mazur**, President of My Madeira Island

Mazur said the waste problem is "not local" and **requires a European response**.

## My Madeira Island Teams Up With Vienna's Green Heroes Austria

During the clean-up, participants also studied the items they had collected. The findings will feed an **online campaign** called 'This Object Should Not Have Existed.'

The campaign targets products such as disposable packaging, **single-use e-cigarettes**, straws and cigarette butts.

## A-WEAR Project Pairs Madeira and Vienna Youth Since January

The clean-up is part of the **A-WEAR project**, short for Waste, Environment, Activism and Responsibility. It is co-financed by the European Union's **Erasmus+ programme**.

Since **January**, the project has brought together young people from Madeira and Vienna. They study waste management, the impact of fast fashion, and ways to build **more sustainable habits**.
