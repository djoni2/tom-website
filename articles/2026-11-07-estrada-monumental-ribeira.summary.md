# Heavy Rain Turns Funchal's Estrada Monumental Into a Stream

**Estrada Monumental**, one of Funchal's main roads, was completely flooded on Saturday near the Anjo roundabout. Video shared on social media shows water running down the street like a river.

Intense and prolonged rain has caused **damage and flooding** across Madeira, according to RTP Madeira. Several other videos of the flooding have also circulated online.

## Days of Warnings Before the Flooding

Civil Protection had already **warned days earlier** of possible urban flooding, fallen trees and drainage problems. The region's weather service, IPMA, extended its yellow rain and wind warnings for Madeira through Sunday.

Forecasters said rain would be **most intense** over the weekend. Gusts could reach 110 km/h in the mountains, and waves up to four metres were expected on the south coast.
