# Funchal Housing Supply Falls One-Third as Sees Biggest Drop

Funchal has seen a **one-third drop** in houses for sale compared to the same period last year. This represents the largest decrease among all district capitals and autonomous regions analysed.

Madeira island as a whole registered a **23% reduction** in available housing stock during the third quarter of 2026. This shows the sharpest decline among the 20 territories evaluated in the idealista.pt data.

:::callout
**Context**: The national housing stock decreased by 4% year-over-year between July and September, continuing a seven-quarter trend of declining availability that began after a brief rise in Q3 2024.
:::

**Funchal's 33% drop** grew faster than other major cities, with Porto showing a 21% reduction and Viana do Castelo a 17% decrease. 

Among regions, only **Porto and Aveiro** came close to Madeira's 23% decline.

**The idealista.pt data shows** both Funchal and Madeira experienced reduced housing availability for sale. 

This happened during a period when the **national market** again announced fewer properties than the previous year.

In the national comparison, **Castelo Branco and Faro** both registered 15% decreases. 

Followed by **Portalegre (-9%)**, Aveiro (-4%), Lisbon and Leiria (-3% each) and Santarém (-1%).

On the increase side, **Vila Real** showed the largest growth at 34%.

Followed by **Guarda (25%)** and Bragança (18%).
