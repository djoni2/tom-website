# CDU Confirms Funchal Bus Operator Cut 45% of Sunday Trips

CDU leader Duarte Martins confirmed on Tuesday that Horários do Funchal, the company that runs Funchal's bus network, has cut **Sunday trips by 45%**. He made the announcement in central Funchal.

The statement backed up a report that Diário de Notícias had already carried in its print edition that same morning. The left-wing coalition said the company now runs **far fewer buses** on Sundays than on a normal weekday. Some routes were dropped entirely, and others now end service early in the evening.

## Report Led Today's Front Page in Diário de Notícias

Diário de Notícias ran the story about the Sunday cuts with heavy play on its front page on **Tuesday, November 17**. CDU's statement in central Funchal confirmed the newspaper's reporting rather than adding new figures of its own.

## Sundays Now Have 1,109 Fewer Trips Than Weekdays

According to CDU, Horários do Funchal runs **1,109 fewer bus trips** on Sundays than on a normal weekday. The party did not give the total number of weekday trips for comparison.

## Eight Routes Were Completely Eliminated

CDU said **eight bus routes** have been completely eliminated on Sundays. It added that this is on top of the overall cut to the number of trips.

## Three in Ten Stops Lose Service Before 21:00

At almost **three in ten stops**, CDU said, the last bus of the day now passes before 21:00. The party argued this leaves people stranded in the evening with no way home.

CDU said the cuts fall hardest on **shift and weekend workers**. It named young people, families and elderly residents with no other transport as well.

The party accused political leaders and the company's management of inconsistency. They promote **sustainable mobility and decarbonization**, CDU said, while cutting the service that makes both possible. That, CDU argued, pushes people toward private cars instead.

## CDU Lists Five Demands to Reverse the Cuts

The party called for the **immediate reversal** of the cuts. It also wants the route network expanded and an end to the early closing of the service.

CDU demanded **more investment** in modernizing the service and increasing its frequency. Its fifth demand is better recognition for transport workers.
