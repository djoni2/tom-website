# Search For Missing Madeira Lifeguard Suspended

João Quintal, a **37-year-old lifeguard**, is still missing five days after a wave allegedly swept him into the sea off Funchal. Authorities called off the search without results on Tuesday evening, just as a second storm bore down on Madeira.

Quintal was on duty at a hotel in Funchal's Lido area when he disappeared. His father has since paid tribute to him online, in a Facebook post that has drawn **thousands of reactions**.

## Lifeguard, 37, Vanished During Sunday's Storm Bruna

Quintal went missing on **Sunday, 8 November**, at around 14:20. He was working as a lifeguard at a hotel in Funchal's Lido district when he was allegedly dragged into the sea by a powerful wave. Storm Bruna was affecting Madeira at the time.

He was 37 years old, from Funchal, and lived in the city. According to the report, he **had two young children**.

## Search Called Off Tuesday Evening Without Results

Crews searched for Quintal's body by **sea, land and air**. The Funchal Lifeguard Station sent crews out by boat.

On land, the Maritime Police Command and Madeira's Operational Command used **a drone** in the search. An Air Force helicopter also searched from above.

The National Maritime Authority confirmed the search was **interrupted without results** by Tuesday evening. From Wednesday, 11 November, it continues only as part of **routine daily coastal patrols**, not as a dedicated operation.

## Father's Tribute Draws Over 3,500 Reactions

Quintal's father, Jorge Quintal, wrote an emotional tribute to his son on Facebook. The post had drawn **more than 3,500 reactions** by the time of reporting. It also drew almost a thousand comments, many offering condolences to the family.

> You left, my son. On my face I still feel your last kiss. You who saved so many, no one saved you. Fate wanted the waves to take you. You left for work and never came back. A wave took you, and we are still waiting for a wave to bring you back, to give you one last kiss. I did not go with you on that wave, but my heart went with it too.

**Jorge Quintal**, father of João Quintal

## Storm Charles Brings 130 km/h Winds Overnight

Storm Bruna, which struck over the weekend, also left **nine people displaced** on the island, besides Quintal's disappearance. Early on Wednesday, a second system, Storm Charles, reached the archipelago. It put Madeira and mainland Portugal under a **red warning**, the most severe level issued by weather authorities.

Winds in Madeira reached **130 km/h** overnight, and Civil Protection logged more than 100 weather-related occurrences before dawn. No damage assessment from Storm Charles was available yet. Access roads into central Funchal were closed as a safety measure.
