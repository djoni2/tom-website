# Man Has Epileptic Seizure on Rua da Infância in Funchal

A man had an **epileptic seizure** in the street in Funchal on Tuesday morning, 3 November. It happened near the CUF clinic on Rua da Infância, the newspaper JM reports.

## Man of About 60 Falls and Suffers a Bleeding Wound

The man, who is **about 60 years old**, fell during the seizure. According to JM, he had a bleeding wound of some size.

## Two Doctors From the Clinic's Emergency Service Come to Help

A passer-by called for help. **Two doctors** from the emergency service of the nearby clinic came to assist him, together with a first-aider.

An **ambulance** was sent to the scene. After the man was immobilised and his vital signs were checked, he was taken to Dr. Nélio Mendonça Hospital in Funchal.

**GNR officers** who happened to be passing helped to manage the traffic in the street.

JM did not report on the **man's condition** after he reached the hospital.
