# Madeira to Inject €2.7 Million Into Funchal Bus Operator

The Regional Government will inject **2.7 million euros** into Horários do Funchal, the company that runs Funchal's bus network. The move is meant to avoid what Portuguese law calls technical bankruptcy. RTP reported the decision on Thursday.

The money is meant to rebalance the company's accounts after a run of losses. The government, which is Horários do Funchal's shareholder, says the step is needed to keep the **public transport service** running.

## 2026 Accounts Set to Close €628,000 in the Red

Horários do Funchal is expected to end the 2026 financial year with a **negative balance** of 628,000 euros. That is based on known and estimated results for the year. The shortfall adds to losses the company has already built up in previous years.

## Accumulated Losses Pass €9 Million

The company's accumulated losses now total **more than 9 million** euros, according to RTP. Combined with the expected 2026 result, the deficit is larger than half of the company's share capital.

## Half-Capital Loss Triggers a Legal Duty to Act

Portugal's Commercial Companies Code sets a clear rule. A company that loses more than **half its share capital** must get its shareholders involved, or risk breaking the law.

RTP reports that Horários do Funchal's losses have crossed that threshold. That is why the Regional Government, as **sole shareholder**, now has to act.

The government says the decision has **already been made**. It points to three reasons: keeping the transport service running, honoring commitments to suppliers, and making sure the service keeps reaching the population.

## Bailout Follows Weeks of Sunday Service Scrutiny

The financial rescue comes **weeks after** Horários do Funchal's Sunday timetable drew public criticism. Earlier in November, opposition party CDU said the company had cut Sunday bus trips sharply compared with weekdays.

The company disputed that days later. It said the gap **matched normal scheduling** rather than a cut.
