# Madeira Wages Rise 5% to 1,558 Euros in Third Quarter

The average worker in Madeira earned **1,558 euros a month** before tax in the third quarter of 2026. That is **5.0% more** than the same period last year, according to provisional figures from the Regional Directorate of Statistics of Madeira (DREM).

The increase covers **gross total monthly pay** per position held. It applies to workers at companies and organizations with their fiscal headquarters in the Autonomous Region of Madeira. Regular pay, which excludes irregular bonuses and overtime, rose faster still, climbing **5.5%** to 1,375 euros. Base pay, the fixed part of a salary, rose **5.4%** to 1,320 euros.

## Inflation Erodes the Gain to Just 0.5% in Real Terms

The 5.0% rise looks smaller once prices are taken into account. Measured against Madeira's Consumer Price Index, total pay rose just **0.5%** in real terms over the year.

Regular pay fared a little better in real terms, up **1.0%**, and base pay rose **0.9%**. The gap between the nominal and real figures shows how much of the wage increase was absorbed by the cost of living on the island.

## The Region Counted 112,700 Jobs, Up Just 0.2%

The figures cover **112,700 positions** in the third quarter, a rise of only **0.2%** compared with the same period of 2025. DREM counts a position for every Social Security beneficiary and Caixa Geral de Aposentações subscriber at an entity based in the Region.

That means the headline pay rise came mostly from **higher wages** rather than from more people working, since the number of jobs barely moved.

## Support Services Pay Leads Gains at 9.2%

Not every sector gained equally. "Administrative and support service activities" saw the sharpest rise in total pay, up **9.2%** year-on-year, followed by construction, up **7.7%**.

Company size mattered too. Firms with **50 to 99 workers** saw pay rise **8.9%**, while the smallest firms, with 5 to 9 workers, saw a **6.6%** rise. By technology and knowledge intensity, "low industrial-technology" companies posted a **7.4%** increase and companies in "low knowledge-intensive services" rose **6.8%**.

## Private Sector Pay Rises 5.6%, Ahead of Public Sector's 4.0%

In Madeira, average pay in the private sector reached **1,397 euros**, up **5.6%** year-on-year. That was faster than the public sector, where Public Administration pay reached **2,033 euros**, up **4.0%**.

The same pattern held nationally. Private sector pay in Portugal rose **5.1%**, while public sector pay rose **4.1%**.

## Madeira Pay Still Trails the National Average by 8.1%

Despite the rise, Madeira's average pay remains well below the rest of the country. The Region's **1,558 euros** sits **8.1% below** the national average of **1,696 euros**.

Nationally, pay rose **4.9%** in nominal terms, from 1,617 euros in September 2025. In real terms, adjusted for inflation, the national rise was **1.5%**, three times Madeira's real increase of 0.5%.

## Energy Workers Earn 3,502 Euros, the Region's Top Wage

One sector stands far above the rest, both in Madeira and nationally. In September 2026, companies in "production and distribution of electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning" paid the **highest average total pay** of any activity.

Workers in that sector earned **3,502 euros a month** in Madeira, compared with **3,076 euros** nationally. By company size, Madeira's best-paying firms were those with **100 to 249 workers**, averaging **1,816 euros**. Nationally, the best-paying band was larger firms, with 250 to 499 workers, averaging **1,945 euros**.
