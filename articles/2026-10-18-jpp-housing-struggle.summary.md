# Youth Party Warns Housing Crisis Continues

By **Mat**

## Ongoing Struggle for Youth

The Juventude do Juntos Pelo Povo (JPP) warns that housing access remains a critical challenge for **young people** in Madeira.

**This is fifty years** after the April 25th revolution.

**Young graduates** with degrees often consider emigrating to afford basic living conditions.

The indicators show that housing access is **one of the biggest** challenges for Madeiran youth.
They face **increasing difficulties** achieving independence and building a life on their own island.

> "But is it selfish to demand a salary that lasts the month? Is it selfish to want a house without compromising almost all of your income? Before criticizing those who protest, the Government must explain what it is doing to solve these problems"

**[Diogo Monteiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diogo_Monteiro)**, Vice-President of Juventude JPP

Housing prices rose 17.6% in 2025 according to INE data, with median rents for new contracts reaching €12.78 per square meter in Q2 2026.
**Many young people** delay financial independence because incomes fail to match living costs, not due to lack of work ethic.

The party demands **more public housing** at affordable costs, better salaries, and action against precarious work.
They also seek **policies enabling** youth to remain in Madeira and build their futures.
They warn Portugal cannot continue educating youth only to lose them to emigration.
