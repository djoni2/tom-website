# Videos Show Flights Abort Landings in Storm Bruna

Strong winds forced pilots into failed landing attempts at Madeira Airport on Saturday. Dozens of flights were cancelled because of **Storm Bruna**, and videos of the aborted approaches spread online.

Local plane-spotters filmed **two aircraft** pulling up from their final approach. A separate clip, filmed by a passenger, shows the same kind of manoeuvre from inside the cabin.

## Condor and Easyjet Aircraft Both Abort Landings

A video published by **Madeira Aviation Spotting** shows a Condor aircraft and an easyJet aircraft both abandoning their landing attempts at Madeira Airport. Both planes climbed away instead of touching down.

The manoeuvre, known as a **go-around**, is used when conditions make a safe landing too risky. Strong, gusty winds around the airport were behind Saturday's attempts.

## A Second Video Shows a Passenger's Own Aborted Landing

Other footage of the day's tense moments was also shared online. One clip was filmed by **a passenger** aboard a flight that aborted its landing in Madeira.

The video captures the moment from **inside the cabin**, as the aircraft pulled out of its approach rather than touching down.

## Storm Bruna Had Grounded 37 Flights by Saturday Afternoon

By Saturday afternoon, **37 flights** had been cancelled at Madeira International Airport, according to ANA Aeroportos, the airport operator. The total covered **20 arrivals, 17 departures**.

The cancellations were linked to Storm Bruna, which brought worsening weather to the archipelago over the weekend. Affected airlines included **TAP, easyJet, Transavia**, British Airways, Ryanair, Jet2 and Enter Air.

## Gusts Could Reach 130 Km/H Through Sunday

Portugal's weather service, **IPMA**, forecast persistent and sometimes heavy rain between Saturday and Sunday. Wind was expected to reach 100 km/h in low-lying areas and **130 km/h** in the highlands.

ANA Aeroportos warned passengers to check with their airline **before going** to the airport. The warning pointed to conditions that could keep affecting departures and arrivals in the days ahead.
