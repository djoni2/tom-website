# Funchal Posts Portugal's Sharpest Room Rent Increase

Renting a room in a shared house in Funchal became far more expensive over the past year. The median price of a room **climbed 22%** between the summer of 2025 and the summer of 2026, the steepest rise of any municipality in the country, according to an analysis by the property platform idealista.

Nationally, room rents rose **8%** in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the same period a year earlier. Measured against the previous quarter, prices rose 5% across Portugal as a whole.

## Funchal Posts the Steepest Rise, Up 22% From Last Year

The idealista analysis covered **20 municipalities**, each with a stable base of at least 30 room listings on the platform. Prices rose in 16 of them.

Funchal's 22% increase was well ahead of the next-steepest rise, in Coimbra, where room rents climbed **18%**. Vila Real followed at 16%, then Beja at 12% and Santarém at 10%.

## National Room Rents Climb 8% in the Past Year

Behind Funchal and Coimbra, **Lisbon** stands out for a different reason. As Portugal's capital and already its most expensive city for shared housing, rents there rose just 1% over the year, well below the national pace.

Only two municipalities saw prices fall: **Viana do Castelo**, down 15%, and Aveiro, down 1%. Castelo Branco and Portalegre were the only two where room rents held steady.

## A Room in Funchal Now Costs 540 Euros a Month

Beyond the rate of change, idealista also tracked the actual **median price** of a room in each city for the summer of 2026. Lisbon remains the most expensive place to rent a room in Portugal, at 602 euros a month.

Funchal comes second nationally, at **540 euros** a month, ahead of Porto at 479 euros and Ponta Delgada at 444 euros. A room in the Madeiran capital now costs more than in any mainland city except Lisbon.

## Lisbon Tops the List at 602 Euros, Bragança Lowest at 220

At the other end of the scale, **Bragança** and Guarda remain the cheapest places in Portugal to rent a room, at 220 and 224 euros a month. Three other cities, Vila Real, Viseu, and the pair of Castelo Branco and Portalegre, still have rooms for under 300 euros.

**Every other city** on the list, including Funchal, now sits above that threshold.

## Guarda Leads Quarterly Gains With a 12% Jump

Measured against the spring of 2026 rather than a year earlier, the national picture looks **calmer**. Room rents rose in 11 of the 20 municipalities and held steady in three.

**Guarda** posted the largest quarterly increase, up 12%, followed by Beja at 11% and Lisbon at 6%. Bragança rose 5%, Portalegre 4% and Coimbra 3%.

## Funchal Rents Ease 5% From Spring to Summer

Funchal's rise wasn't a straight line. Between spring and summer 2026, the city's room rents actually **fell 5%**, the same quarterly drop recorded in Évora. Viana do Castelo posted the steepest quarterly fall, down 20%.

That dip does not cancel out the year-on-year increase. Funchal's rooms were **still 22% more expensive** than a year earlier, even after cooling off from spring.

Room-sharing in Portugal is **no longer just** a student arrangement, idealista notes. Young professionals, workers posted away from home, and separated people increasingly rent a single room rather than a whole home, and some families have turned to the option too, as rents and house prices have climbed across the country.
