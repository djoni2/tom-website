# Madeira Reopens 20 Hiking Trails, Keeps 19 Shut After Storm

Madeira's Institute of Forests and Nature Conservation, known as **IFCN**, said on Thursday afternoon that **20 classified hiking trails** are now passable. Another **19 trails** remain closed, based on checks carried out so far.

IFCN said the list could still change as further checks are completed. The closures follow **[Depression Charles](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/depression-charles-130-kmh-gusts-madeira)**, which brought gusts of up to 130 km/h and heavy rain to the island between Tuesday night and Wednesday.

> The decision to keep some trails closed also results from persistent problems in the access points to the start of the trails, as well as unstable ground conditions, following the effects of the depression that hit the regional territory.

**IFCN**

IFCN also asked the public to help **identify and report** any abnormal situations on the trails. It named fallen rocks, branches or trees blocking the paths, or anything else that could put hikers at risk.

## Vereda do Areeiro Stays Shut in Two Separate Stretches

The **Vereda do Areeiro** (PR 1) remains closed in **two separate sections**. IFCN lists both the one-way route and the stretch up to the Pedra Rija viewpoint as still shut.

## Porto Santo Keeps One Trail Closed, Reopens the Rest

On Porto Santo, **two routes are passable** again. They are the Vereda do Pico Branco e Terra Chã (PR 1) and the Vereda do Pico do Castelo (PR 2).

One Porto Santo trail **stays closed**: the Levada do Pico do Castelo (PR 3).
