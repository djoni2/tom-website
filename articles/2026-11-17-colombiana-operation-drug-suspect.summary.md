# Colombiana Operation Dismantles Major Madeira Drug Network

A police operation codenamed **Colombiana** has dismantled a major drug supply line into Madeira. Portugal's Public Prosecutor's Office said on Tuesday that one of the main suspects has been placed in **pretrial detention**.

The Public Prosecutor's Office for the Madeira district said the operation followed raids carried out by the PSP on **Thursday and Friday**. Officers searched properties in Funchal and Santa Cruz.

## Suspect Negotiated Drug Deals by the Kilo

According to the prosecutor's note, the suspect built contacts with people on **mainland Portugal and abroad**. Through them, they negotiated the purchase of large quantities of drugs.

The deals ran into **several kilos** at a time. They were struck in person or over social media, and the drugs were then shipped to Madeira by regular mail.

## Suspect Also Supplied Other Drug Networks

Prosecutors said the suspect did not just sell directly to consumers. The individual also supplied drugs to **other criminal networks**, playing a significant role in stocking the regional market.

> With the detention and pretrial detention of this individual, a very significant source of the introduction and distribution of cocaine, hashish, marijuana, ecstasy and MDMA in the Autonomous Region of Madeira has been dismantled.
**Public Prosecutor's Office**, Madeira district

## Five Arrests Made in Flagrante Delito

The Public Prosecutor's Office confirmed a total of **five arrests** in flagrante delito. The charges were drug trafficking and illegal possession of a weapon.

Two of those detained appeared before a judge for a first interrogation. One was placed in **pretrial detention**.

The other must report **daily to the police** station nearest their home. They are also barred from contacting certain people and from visiting certain places.

## Earlier Raids Seized 400 Ecstasy Pills

As the Diário had already reported the previous Saturday, the PSP broke up a group that had imported recreational drugs from other European countries. The group had done so for **at least two years**.

The drugs were destined mainly for sale in nightlife venues. According to the PSP's Madeira Regional Command, officers seized **400 ecstasy pills**, equal to 2,161 individual doses.

Officers also seized 505.46 grams of hashish. Another 352.75 grams, allegedly sold as **cocaine**, corresponded to about 1,763 doses.

## Military-Grade Round Found and Destroyed in São Roque

The operation mobilized about **50 PSP officers**. It covered searches of several homes between Funchal and Caniço.

One search, in São Roque, turned up a round of **military-caliber ammunition** with anti-aircraft characteristics. Specialized units were called in, and the round was destroyed in a controlled manner.

The investigation is continuing under the **1st Section of Funchal** of the Department of Criminal Investigation and Prosecution (DIAP) for the Madeira district. The PSP's Funchal Criminal Investigation Squad is supporting the case.
