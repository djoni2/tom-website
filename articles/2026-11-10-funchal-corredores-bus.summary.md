# Funchal Opens Bus Lanes to Speed Up Storm Exit

The **Funchal city council** opened the city's bus lanes to general traffic on Tuesday. The move, coordinated with the **Public Security Police (PSP)**, is meant to help people leave the city centre faster as bad weather worsens.

## Lanes Normally Reserved for Public Transport Open in Funchal

The council said the change would take effect **within moments** of its announcement. Lanes usually reserved for buses would open to all traffic, with the PSP coordinating the change on the ground.

## Move Follows Worsening Conditions Tied to Depression Charles

The measure comes as weather conditions worsen with the approach of **depression Charles**. The council said the goal is to **improve traffic flow** and allow a quicker exit from central Funchal.

Storm Charles has already prompted **[a red rain warning](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/ipma-red-alert-south-coast-mountains-madeira)** for Madeira's south coast. It runs from Tuesday night into Wednesday morning, with mountain gusts forecast up to **140 km/h**.

## Authorities Urge Residents to Limit Travel

Alongside the bus lane measure, authorities are **urging residents to reduce** unnecessary travel and to adopt precautionary behaviour while the bad weather continues.
