# Charity Urges Madeira to Protect Pets From Storm Charles

Ajuda a Alimentar Cães, an animal welfare group, is urging pet owners across Madeira to bring their animals indoors. The appeal comes as **storm Charles** approaches the islands with heavy rain, cold and strong wind.

The group posted its warning on Tuesday. It flagged the danger facing dogs and cats left **chained outside**, or kept on balconies and in yards without proper shelter.

> Not only humans will be affected.

**Ajuda a Alimentar Cães**

The charity argues that doghouses alone do not offer **enough protection** once wind and rain turn severe. It wants owners to move animals into safe indoor spaces, rather than leaving them tied up or exposed on balconies, yards or open land.

> Animals cannot protect themselves, and they depend entirely on us.

**Ajuda a Alimentar Cães**

## Red Rain Warning Runs Until 06:00 on Wednesday

The appeal follows a **red rain warning** that Portugal's weather service, IPMA, placed on Madeira's south coast. **[Câmara de Lobos](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/camara-de-lobos-emergency-shelters-storm-charles)** is already preparing emergency shelters ahead of the storm.

The warning covers strong, persistent rain starting at 21:00 on Tuesday and ending at **06:00 on Wednesday**. That is the same window in which the charity says pets are most exposed.

## South Coast Winds May Gust to 100 KM/H

An **orange wind warning** also applies to the south coast, running from 21:00 on Tuesday to midday on Wednesday. Gusts there could reach **100 km/h**.

## Mountain Areas Face a Red Alert at 140 KM/H

Higher ground faces a sharper risk. IPMA has placed **[Madeira's mountain areas](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/ipma-red-alert-south-coast-mountains-madeira)** under a red wind alert, with gusts forecast up to **140 km/h**.

These are the conditions behind the charity's appeal. Animals left outdoors in such wind and rain have **little chance** to shelter themselves.

## Charity Asks Neighbors Not to Just Film and Walk Away

Ajuda a Alimentar Cães also appealed to the wider public. It asked residents who spot a neglected animal to **approach the owner** and offer help, instead of simply taking photographs.

> If every person who sees neglected animals does not stay indifferent and tries to help in some way, we can improve conditions for many animals.

**Ajuda a Alimentar Cães**

The group closed its appeal with a call to **protect animals and people** as the bad weather takes hold across Madeira.
