# Madeira's Trade Surplus Shrinks to €54.3 Million

Madeira's trade balance with foreign countries stayed positive in the first nine months of 2026, but the surplus narrowed sharply. The region recorded a surplus of **€54.3 million** between January and September. The Regional Directorate of Statistics of Madeira (DREM) released the preliminary figures on Monday, based on data from Portugal's national statistics institute, INE.

That surplus is **63.2% lower** than the €147.7 million recorded in the same nine months of 2025.

## Exports Fall 8.8% to €365.4 Million

Companies based in the region exported goods worth **€365.4 million** between January and September 2026. That was a drop of 8.8% from the same period of 2025.

By product, food was the leading export category, worth **€67.2 million**. Machinery and equipment followed at €62.5 million, according to DREM's detailed trade tables.

## Imports Climb 23.1% to €311.1 Million

At the same time, imports into Madeira reached **€311.1 million**. That was 23.1% higher than in the first nine months of 2025, growing faster than exports fell.

## Import Coverage Ratio Drops to 117.4%

The shift between falling exports and rising imports shows up in the import coverage ratio. That ratio measures how much of the region's imports its exports pay for. The ratio fell to **117.4%** in the first nine months of 2026, down from 158.4% a year earlier.

The geography of Madeira's trade stayed lopsided between January and September 2026. Most of the region's exports, **72.1%**, went to countries outside the European Union, while the EU took the remaining 27.9%.

Imports followed the opposite pattern. The European Union supplied **80.8%** of everything Madeira brought in from abroad, with third countries accounting for the other 19.2%.

## Angola Buys 48.7% of All Exports

Angola was by far Madeira's biggest export market in the first nine months of 2026. The African country bought **€177.8 million** worth of goods, or 48.7% of everything the region exported.

Together, Madeira's top five export markets took **77.7%** of all exports in the period. The next four destinations combined, Italy, the United Kingdom, Spain and France, still added up to less than Angola alone.

## Spain Supplies 29.9% of All Imports

Spain was the region's largest source of imports in the first nine months of 2026. Madeira bought **€93.0 million** worth of goods from there, close to 30% of everything it imported.
