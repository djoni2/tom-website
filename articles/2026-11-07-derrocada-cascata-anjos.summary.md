# Landslide Hits Area Near Cascata Dos Anjos In Ponta Do Sol

A landslide struck the area around the **Cascata dos Anjos** waterfall in Ponta do Sol on 6 November. The site has since been signposted as a precaution.

## What We Know So Far

A council member from **Ponta do Sol** confirmed the landslide to the newspaper DIÁRIO. No other details about the event have been released.

Authorities have **not yet said** what caused the landslide or whether any roads or properties were affected. The area stays signposted while the situation is assessed.
