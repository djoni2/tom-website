# Madeira Inflation Stays at 4.6% for Second Month

Madeira's annual inflation rate held at **4.6% in October**, the same level as September, the regional statistics office said Wednesday. The rate stayed above mainland Portugal's national average of 3.8%.

The Direção Regional de Estatística da Madeira, known as DREM, also reported that energy prices **rose 20.3%** compared with October last year. Energy was the single biggest driver of the region's higher cost of living.

The 4.6% figure is the year-on-year rate, comparing prices in October 2026 with October 2025. A separate measure, the **12-month average rate**, stayed at 3.8% in October, unchanged from September. Core inflation, which strips out unprocessed food and energy, held at 3.3%.

Within that 12-month average, services kept **rising faster than goods**. Services prices increased 5.2% on average, while goods rose 2.6%. Transport and the restaurant and accommodation sector posted the steepest average increases, at 7.4% and 7.1%.

Information and communication was the only class with a **notably negative average rate**, falling 1.4%.

## Energy Prices Surge 20.3% Over the Past Year

Energy products showed the **sharpest yearly increase** of any group, up 20.3% compared with October 2025. DREM listed energy as the standout figure among the aggregates it tracks.

On the same year-on-year basis, **services rose 6.2%** and **goods rose 3.2%**. Both figures are higher than their 12-month average counterparts, showing prices accelerated more recently.

## Transport Costs Climb 12.1% in Yearly Comparison

Transport led all categories in the year-on-year comparison, with prices **up 12.1%** from October 2025. It was also the single largest contributor to the region's overall inflation rate.

DREM said transport added **2.3 percentage points** to the 4.6% annual rate, the biggest contribution of any category. Restaurants and accommodation added a further 0.8 percentage points.

## Restaurant and Hotel Prices Rise 7.6% Annually

Restaurants and accommodation services cost **7.6% more** than a year earlier, the second-steepest annual rise after transport. Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels rose 5.5% over the same period.

## Clothing Is the Only Category to Fall, Down 2.6%

Clothing and footwear was the **only category to fall**, with prices down 2.6% from October 2025. Every other class DREM measured rose over the same period.

## Monthly Consumer Prices Fall 0.7% in October

Measured month on month, Madeira's consumer price index **fell 0.7% in October**, reversing a 1.2% rise in September. Nationally, prices moved the opposite way, rising 0.2% in October after a 1.2% increase the month before.

## Average Rents Climb 6.7% From a Year Earlier

The average rent per square metre of usable floor space in Madeira rose **0.4% in October** compared with September, and 6.7% compared with October 2025. Both figures eased slightly from September, when rents had risen 0.6% monthly and 6.9% annually.
