# Madeira Tests Emergency Alert SMS Ahead of Storm

Madeira's Civil Protection service announced on Tuesday that it would test a **preventive SMS alert system**. The message goes to every phone in the region during the afternoon. The test comes as the island braces for a red weather warning linked to **[Depression Charles](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/depression-charles-130-kmh-gusts-madeira)**.

## Alert Goes Out in Two Languages

Civil Protection made the announcement at a press conference. The test message was due to go out during **Tuesday afternoon**, sent to all mobile phones present in the region.

The text appears in **Portuguese for national numbers** and in **English for foreign numbers**, so visitors on the island also receive the warning.

## Public Information Line Opens for the Storm

Alongside the SMS test, authorities are setting up a **public information line**. It will run through the bad weather period to answer questions from the public.

The test comes as IPMA, Portugal's weather service, has a **[red alert](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/ipma-red-alert-south-coast-mountains-madeira)** in place for Madeira's south coast and mountains. It runs from Tuesday night into Wednesday morning, with gusts of up to **140 km/h** forecast in the highlands.
