# Bad Weather Cancels Lobo Marinho Trips on 7 and 8 November

The Lobo Marinho ferry between Madeira and Porto Santo will **not sail this weekend**. Porto Santo Line (PSL) said on Wednesday that the trips on Saturday, 7 November, and Sunday, 8 November, are cancelled.

The company gave the **weather forecast** as the reason:

> "the bad weather conditions forecast for the Madeira archipelago, which could put the safety of its passengers and the ship at risk"

**Porto Santo Line**, operator of the Lobo Marinho

PSL did **not say** whether extra trips will be added before or after the weekend.

## How to Contact Porto Santo Line

For more information, passengers can call **+351 291 210 300** or send a WhatsApp message to +351 962 025 500. The email address is infopsl@gruposousa.pt.

The phone and WhatsApp lines are open **Monday to Friday** from 09:00 to 19:00. On Saturdays and Sundays they are open from 10:00 to 15:00 and from 15:30 to 18:00. They are closed on public holidays.

## Ticket Offices in Funchal and Porto Santo

Passengers can also go to **one of the offices**:

- **Avenida do Mar e das Comunidades Madeirenses, 20, Funchal**: Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 18:00
- **Estrada Monumental, 175 C, Funchal**: every day, 09:00 to 20:00
- **Funchal port (Cais do Funchal)**: every day, 10:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 19:00
- **Rua D. Estevão de Alencastre, Loja 6/7, Porto Santo**: Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00
