# Ronaldo's Mother Celebrates Portugal's Loss to Denmark

**Dolores Aveiro**, the mother of **[Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)**, said she was glad to see Portugal lose. She made the comments after the national team fell **4-1** to Denmark, its first defeat since her son's retirement from the Selecao.

Aveiro linked her reaction to **how Ronaldo was treated** before he ended his international career. She accused the federation of mishandling a tribute to him and accused his former teammates of failing to respect him.

## Portugal Fell 4-1 to Denmark in Copenhagen

Portugal suffered a heavy **4-1** defeat to Denmark, a result that left the Selecao reeling. It was a difficult start to life without their long-time captain, who retired from international football earlier this month.

Speaking at an event at **Sporting CP**, Aveiro did not hide her satisfaction at the result.

> The defeat was well deserved, and I was happy about it.
**Dolores Aveiro**

The comment sent shockwaves through Portuguese sports media, given her son's status as the country's **greatest-ever player**.

## Mother Says Ronaldo's 234 Caps Deserved a Personal Send-off

Ronaldo earned **234 caps** and scored **146 goals** for Portugal over a 23-year international career that began in **August 2003**. Aveiro's anger focused partly on a recent tribute organized for him by the federation, which she felt was handled poorly.

She argued the striker, who plays for **[Al-Nassr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Nassr_FC)**, deserved a more personal farewell.

> The tribute should have been paid in his presence.
**Dolores Aveiro**

## She Accuses Teammates of Disrespecting the Five-Time Ballon d'Or Winner

Aveiro also attacked the players who shared the dressing room with her son during his final months with the squad. She accused them of failing to support a player who led Portugal to **Euro 2016** and Nations League titles.

> With the national team, he was not respected, not even by his team-mates.
**Dolores Aveiro**

The claim suggests tension behind the scenes during the final months of Ronaldo's international career. The **five-time Ballon d'Or winner** ended that career earlier this month, after a public falling out with head coach **[Jorge Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Jesus)**.

Aveiro said only **one club** truly understands her son's legacy.

> It's here, at Sporting, that we're going to pay tribute to him. Here, they recognise his worth.
**Dolores Aveiro**

The comment pointed to **Sporting CP**, the club where Ronaldo's professional career began, as the only institution capable of showing him proper respect. It further isolates the current national team setup, including Jesus.

## Jorge Jesus, 72, Declines to Respond to the Comments

Jesus was asked about Aveiro's remarks during a press conference. The **72-year-old** manager, whose side has struggled defensively since Ronaldo's exit, refused to engage in a public dispute.

> Now do I have to respond to Cristiano Ronaldo's mother? My mother too. Cristiano Ronaldo's mother is a lady I respect greatly. A mother is still a mother, there's nothing you can do about it. I understand her statements.
**Jorge Jesus**
