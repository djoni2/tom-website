# Four From Madeira Parish Council Charged With Embezzlement

The Public Prosecutor's Office has charged a **former president** and three members of a parish council in Madeira. The charges include embezzlement, abuse of office and illegal economic participation in business by holders of political office, RTP Madeira reported on 3 November.

The prosecutors did not say **which parish council** the four defendants belonged to.

## Alleged Offences Took Place Between 2017 and 2021

According to the Madeira district prosecutor's office, the acts took place **between 2017 and 2021**. That covers two terms of local government, 2013-2017 and 2017-2021.

## Former President Faces 28 Counts of Embezzlement

According to the charges, the then **parish council president** committed nine crimes of embezzlement by a holder of political office. These were allegedly committed together with another council member, who has since died.

The prosecutors say the former president also committed another **19 such crimes** alone.

Three other members of the council's executive are charged **jointly with one count** of the same crime.

## Public Money Allegedly Paid for Trips, Meals and Stays

The case concerns the alleged **misuse of public money** to pay for trips, meals, travel and stays, for the benefit of the defendants and others.

Investigators also looked at the **hiring of a worker** and the distribution of social support. Other acts examined were spending on the parish's festivities, support for an association and the diversion of computer equipment.

## Prosecutors Seek Compensation on Behalf of the Parish

The prosecutors have also filed a **claim for compensation** against the defendants, on behalf of the parish.

The investigation was led by the Funchal section of the **prosecution department** (DIAP). It had help from the Judiciary Police in Madeira and technical advisers from the Attorney General's Office.

Being charged is not a conviction. The defendants are presumed **innocent** until a court rules otherwise.
