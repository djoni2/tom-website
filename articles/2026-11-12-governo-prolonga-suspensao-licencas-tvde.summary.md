# Madeira Extends TVDE Licence Freeze by Four Months

Madeira's **regional government** has extended the suspension on new TVDE operator and driver licences by four months. The Council of the Government made the decision on Thursday.

The freeze blocks **new operators and drivers** from entering the TVDE sector, the Portuguese term for paid passenger transport in unmarked private cars. It covers the apps that compete with taxis on the island.

## Freeze on New TVDE Licences Began in September 2025

The regional government first **ordered the freeze** in September 2025. It has been kept in place, and extended more than once, ever since.

## Licensed Fleet Already Grew to About 800 Vehicles

While the freeze has run, the **licensed TVDE fleet** in Madeira has climbed to about 800 vehicles. No new operators or drivers have been allowed to join the sector during that time.

Times of Madeira previously reported on the **sector's growth** and the government's plan to [extend the freeze again](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/madeira-to-extend-freeze-on-new-tvde-licences-again).

## Study on TVDE and Taxis Presented on 4 November

The plan to extend the suspension again was first signalled the **previous week**, on 4 November. Pedro Rodrigues, the regional secretary for Equipment and Infrastructure, raised it at a meeting with representatives of the transport sector.

At that meeting, the secretary presented the **conclusions of a study** on TVDE and taxi activity. It assessed their impact on mobility and transport planning in Madeira.

## Previous Suspension Had Been Due to End on 13 November

Before Thursday's decision, the suspension was due to **expire on 13 November**, the day after the Council of the Government met. Counting four months from that date would put the new deadline at around mid-March 2027, though the government has not announced an exact end date.
