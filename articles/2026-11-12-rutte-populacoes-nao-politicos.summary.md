# Rutte Says Ukraine Remarks Targeted Public, Not Leaders

**[Mark Rutte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Rutte)**, the secretary-general of **NATO**, said on Thursday that he was referring to the public, not politicians. He had said some people in Portugal and Spain see the war in Ukraine as distant.

## Rutte Defends Original Remarks at Dublin Summit

Rutte made the clarification to reporters as he arrived at the **European Political Community summit** in Dublin. He said he has **no criticism** to make of Portugal or Spain.

He said both countries are doing exactly what is needed. They are **increasing defense spending** and strengthening the production capacity of the defense industry.

> What I have been saying is that, across Europe, and not only in Portugal or Spain, populations - not politicians, but people inside these countries - sometimes have the perception that there is a difference between the eastern countries, like Estonia and Poland, and the rest.

**Mark Rutte**, Secretary-General of NATO

Reporters asked if he planned to meet Portugal's prime minister, **[Luís Montenegro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lu%C3%ADs_Montenegro)**, on the sidelines of the summit to explain his position. Rutte replied: "I don't think I have anything more to explain."

## NATO Chief Points to Divide Between Estonia, Poland and Rest of Europe

Rutte insisted that the perception he described is **not limited** to Portugal and Spain, but happens across Europe as a whole.

He repeated a comparison from **two days earlier**, framing it as a matter of geography rather than commitment.

> And the only thing I am saying is that the newest Russian missiles take only five minutes longer to reach Dublin, Amsterdam or Madrid, Italy or Rome, than Tallinn or Vilnius.

**Mark Rutte**, Secretary-General of NATO

## Original Tuesday Interview Cited Five-Minute Missile Gap to Lisbon

The row began on **Tuesday**, when Rutte gave an interview to a Dutch television station. In it, he accused Spain and Portugal of saying the war in Ukraine **"is far away."**

> Sometimes I tell our allies in Spain or Portugal, who say: it is far away, and what happens in Russia mainly affects Poland.

**Mark Rutte**, Secretary-General of NATO

He added that **the newest Russian missiles** take five minutes longer to reach Lisbon than they do Warsaw.

## Portugal's Foreign Ministry Calls Remarks 'Lamentable'

Portugal's **Ministry of Foreign Affairs**, led by **[Paulo Rangel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_Rangel)**, criticized the Tuesday remarks. It said it regretted that Rutte spoke with such carelessness on the matter.

The ministry wrote on the social media platform **X**. It said Rutte has a duty to know which countries and governments stand firmly beside Ukraine and understand the Russian threat well.

> Portugal, its Government and its people have never had doubts about the right side of History. It is regrettable that the NATO secretary-general speaks with such carelessness about a matter of this seriousness.

**Portuguese Ministry of Foreign Affairs**
