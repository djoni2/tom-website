# Madeira Says TAP Sale Must Not Cut Island Air Links

The President of Madeira's Regional Government says the **TAP privatisation**
cannot put the air links to Madeira and the Azores at risk. The State must
still enforce public service obligations and territorial continuity, he argues,
even with a new shareholder in the company.

:::callout
**Who he is**: Madeira's regional government has been led by
**[Miguel Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Albuquerque)** since
April 2015. He was mayor of Funchal for 19 years before that.
:::

The question is live because the national government has chosen **Lufthansa**
to buy up to 49.9% of the airline. The plan sells 44.9% to a lead investor and
keeps up to **5% for workers**. For now, the State still holds most of the
capital.

## What Happens Next

Choosing Lufthansa does not close the deal. **Final talks** and regulatory
approvals come next, and those steps could stretch the sale out to **2028**.

For Madeira, the central question stays the same: whether TAP will keep a
service that fits **residents' mobility needs** and the region's connection to
the mainland.

The report does **not say** what the agreement promises about the island
routes, and it carries no comment from the national government.
