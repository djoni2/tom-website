# Study Finds Ronaldo Shapes How the World Sees Portugal

**[Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)** is one of Portugal's main image assets, according to a new study. Most people surveyed say the captain has a strong influence on how the world sees the country.

The study was carried out by **ISCTE Executive Education** and the Instituto de Marketing Research. RTP Madeira reported the results on 4 November 2026.

The researchers ran **3,931 surveys** in nine countries between 13 and 18 October. That was a few weeks after Ronaldo [left the national team](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/rooney-says-ronaldo-undermined-portugal-coach-and-team).

## 76% Say Ronaldo Strongly Shapes Portugal's Image Abroad

A member of the study team told RTP Madeira that the result was **very clear** in all nine countries.

> "This finding is very clear in these nine countries, it cuts across all of them, and in our opinion it is extraordinarily significant that 76% of respondents across all these countries consider that Cristiano Ronaldo has a very strong influence on Portugal's image abroad."

**Member of the study team**, speaking to RTP Madeira

## Over 70% Say His Prestige Goes Beyond Football

More than 70% of respondents believe **Ronaldo's prestige goes** beyond football.

Respondents also see him as an **inspiration in football** because of his winning mentality.

## 63% Say Ronaldo Builds the Team's Image, Only 10% the Reverse

The study found that Ronaldo does **much more** for the team than the team does for him.

> "Cristiano contributes much more to shaping the national team's image than the other way round."

**Member of the study team**, speaking to RTP Madeira

In numbers, **63% of people** said Ronaldo creates image for the national team. Only 10% said it is the national team that feeds Ronaldo's image.

## Respondents in Nine Countries Show Less Interest After His Exit

The study also found that the **captain's departure** could have an impact beyond football.

In football terms, the study team found a **direct effect** in the nine countries. It found a significant loss of interest in the national team.

It also found **less emotional involvement** with Portugal and with the national team in particular.

In material terms, respondents showed **less interest in content** about the country in general and about Portuguese football.

Even so, a significant part of the respondents say Ronaldo and **what he has achieved** will remain, even after he leaves the national team.

## 45% of Portuguese Respondents Say He Did Most for the Country's Prestige

In Portugal, 45% of respondents named Ronaldo as the **Portuguese personality** who did most for the country's prestige around the world.

Many would also like to see him as an **ambassador** for the national team.
