# Madeira's Airports Carry 534,600 Passengers in September, Up 2.3%

More people flew in and out of Madeira in September, on **slightly fewer planes**. The region's two airports handled about 534,600 passengers on 3,559 commercial flights, according to the Regional Statistics Office of Madeira (DREM).

That is 2.3% more passengers than in September 2025, but 0.8% fewer aircraft movements. The figures come from the airport operator, ANA - **Aeroportos de Portugal**.

## Madeira Airport Passengers Rise 2.1% to 502,400

At **Madeira Airport**, passenger numbers rose 2.1% to about 502,400. Aircraft movements fell 0.5% to 3,264.

International traffic made up 57.5% of passengers at the main airport, and **domestic traffic** 42.5%.

## Porto Santo Passengers Grow 5.5% With 295 Flights

The smaller **Porto Santo Airport** grew faster. It handled about 32,300 passengers, 5.5% more than a year earlier, even though aircraft movements fell 3.3% to 295.

Domestic flights still dominate on Porto Santo, with 79.6% of passengers. But the share of **international passengers** grew: a year earlier, domestic traffic made up 89.7%.

## Seat Occupancy Climbs to 88.2% From 86.1%

Planes were **fuller** than a year ago. Across both airports, seat occupancy was about 88.2%, up from 86.1% in September 2025.

The change was largest on **Porto Santo**, where occupancy rose from 81.5% to 90.6%. Each plane at Madeira Airport carried 154 passengers on average, up from 150. On Porto Santo, the average rose from 100 to 109.

## Domestic Traffic Rises 3.2%, International 1.6%

Domestic traffic grew faster in September. Passengers on flights within Portugal rose 3.2% to about 239,100. **International traffic** rose 1.6% to about 295,500.

**Scheduled flights** accounted for 93.1% of aircraft movements and 95.5% of passengers, both slightly higher than a year earlier.

## UK Accounts for 26.8% of International Traffic This Year

From January to September, the two airports counted about **4.47 million passengers**, not counting those in transit. Of these, 56.5% travelled on international flights and 43.5% on flights within Portugal.

The **United Kingdom** was the top country for international flights, with 26.8%, followed by Germany (19.7%), France (11.0%), Poland (9.0%) and the Netherlands (6.4%).

In the **first nine months**, passenger numbers rose 3.0% and aircraft movements 1.5% compared with the same period of 2025. Madeira's airports had already set records in [2024](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/2024-5-million-passengers-madeira) and saw [international traffic grow](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/madeira-airports-see-174-rise-in-international-traffic-in-2025) strongly in 2025.
