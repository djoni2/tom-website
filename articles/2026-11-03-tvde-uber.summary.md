# Madeira TVDE Association Signs Advertising Deal With Uber

The **Madeira TVDE Association** has signed a cooperation protocol with Uber Portugal, the newspaper JM reported on 3 November. The association says the deal will create new sources of income for companies in the region.

TVDE is the Portuguese name for **paid passenger transport** in unmarked private cars, the sector in which ride-hailing apps such as Uber operate.

## First Phase Puts Ads on Both Sides of TVDE Cars

In a **first phase**, the partnership will start an advertising project in TVDE cars that operate in Madeira. The sides of the cars will be used as space for adverts.

## Companies to Receive 100 Euros a Month for Each Car Taking Part

Companies that join will be paid **100 euros a month** for each car in the project, according to JM.

The source does not say **how many cars** or companies are expected to take part, or when the adverts will first appear.

## Association Wants the Deal to Reach Companies Across Madeira

According to the **association**, the initiative "is born from a clear vision":

> "to turn TVDE activity into an opportunity to create value, providing new sources of income to regional business owners and contributing to the economic sustainability of their operations"

**Madeira TVDE Association**, quoted by JM

The association hopes the cooperation will reach **as many companies** as possible. It sees the deal as a chance to build links between the platforms and business owners, and to make the sector stronger and economically sustainable.

The association's **president** said the partnership "constitutes an initiative of interest to the regional economy and to all agents linked to mobility and passenger transport in the Autonomous Region of Madeira".

## Partners Plan Further Projects for the TVDE Sector in Madeira

The association and Uber Portugal are also working on **new projects** and initiatives. They say these could bring concrete benefits to the whole TVDE sector in Madeira in the future. JM gave **no details** of them.

The deal comes after **a long dispute** over TVDE rules on the island.
