# Marina do Funchal's New Commercial Area Nears Opening

The Marina do Funchal is getting a **new look** after a renovation process. A mix of restaurants, bars and shops is moving into the site. Some units are already installed, while others are still finishing construction, according to a report by RTP Madeira.

## Marina do Funchal Gains 33 New Commercial Spaces

All of the new spaces have already been allocated through **public tenders**, for a total of **33** units. Some of the shops will also offer public entertainment, alongside other services meant to draw more people to the marina.

The spaces were awarded strictly on the strength of each **bidder's proposal** in the public tender. No other factor played a role, according to the report.

## Four Restaurants and Three Bars Join the Marina

The new commercial area includes **four restaurants** and **three bars**, plus a number of shops. Some units are already open, while others are still being finished.

The marina will also have a **supermarket**, a mini-market and a laundry, among other services. These are meant for the boats that stop and dock there, but also for visitors and the general public.

## All Spaces Set to Open by the End of November

The expectation is that the **entire area** will be up and running by the end of the month. Several entertainment initiatives are also planned, to attract visitors and showcase the renovated space.
