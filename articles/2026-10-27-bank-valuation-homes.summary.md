# Bank Valuations of Madeira Homes Rise 14.3% in a Year

Banks valued homes in Madeira at a median of **2,642 euros** per square metre in September 2026. That is 14.3% more than a year earlier, or 331 euros more per square metre.

Compared with August, the value rose **0.6%**, or 17 euros. The figures were released on Tuesday by the regional statistics office, DREM, using data from the national institute INE.

## Houses Rise Faster Than Flats

The biggest jump was in **detached houses**. Their median valuation reached 2,347 euros per square metre, up 20.3% in a year.

Apartments were valued at **2,918 euros** per square metre, up 15.3%. In one month, houses rose 0.7% and apartments 0.2%.

## Funchal Passes 3,000 Euros

In **Funchal**, the median valuation was 3,037 euros per square metre. That is 16.6% more than a year ago and 4.3% more than in August.

**Santa Cruz** reached 2,707 euros, up 21% in a year and 3.8% in a month. It had the biggest yearly rise of the three municipalities with enough data to publish.

In **Câmara de Lobos**, the value was 2,502 euros, up 12.9% in a year and 6.5% in a month.

## Fourth Highest in Portugal

Across Portugal, the median was **2,278 euros** per square metre, up 14.2% in a year. Madeira ranks fourth of nine regions, behind Greater Lisbon (3,452 euros), the Algarve (2,953 euros) and the Setúbal Peninsula (2,845 euros).

Fewer homes were valued, though. Banks carried out **614 valuations** in September, 51 fewer than in August, a drop of 7.7%. The article links this to a fall in sales.

Compared with September 2025, the number of valuations fell **2.7%**. Of the total, 267 were apartments and 347 were houses.
