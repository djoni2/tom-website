# Fact-Check: Madeira Does Not Have Portugal's Lowest Life Expectancy

People in Madeira still live **shorter lives** than the average in Portugal. But is the island the region of the country where life is shortest? A fact-check by the newspaper DIÁRIO looked at the official numbers and rated that claim as **false**.

The answer comes from the latest mortality tables published by [Statistics Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Portugal) (INE), which cover the **three years** from 2023 to 2025.

## Madeira's Life Expectancy at Birth Stands at 80.06 Years

According to INE, a baby born in the Autonomous Region of Madeira can expect to live **80.06 years**. That is 1.79 years less than the national average, which INE puts at 81.85 years.

Madeira is one of the Portuguese regions with the **lowest figures**. Even so, it is not in last place.

## The Azores Rank Last in Portugal at 78.69 Years

That position belongs to the Autonomous Region of the **Azores**, where life expectancy at birth is 78.69 years.

This means that, according to the official estimates, people in Madeira can expect to live **1.37 years longer** than people in the Azores.

## Northern Portugal Leads the Country With 82.31 Years

At the other end of the table is the **North region**, which has the highest life expectancy in the country, at 82.31 years.

The gap between the North and Madeira is **2.25 years**. Compared with the Azores, the difference reaches 3.62 years.

## Madeira Gained 0.49 Years Compared With 2022-2024

The figures also show that life expectancy in Madeira is **going up**. Compared with the previous three-year period, 2022-2024, the regional figure grew by 0.49 years, or about six months.

The gain was larger among **men**, whose life expectancy rose by about seven months. Among women, the increase was around four months.

## Madeiran Women Expected to Live 82.68 Years, Men 76.83

Women in Madeira still live **much longer** than men. Life expectancy at birth for women in the region is now estimated at 82.68 years, against 76.83 years for men.

That is a gap of **almost six years** between the sexes.

## Fact-Check Verdict: The Claim Is Rated False

DIÁRIO's conclusion was clear. Madeira is among the regions of Portugal where **life expectancy** at birth is lowest, but it does not hold last place. On the latest INE data, that place belongs to the Azores, so the claim is **rated false**.
