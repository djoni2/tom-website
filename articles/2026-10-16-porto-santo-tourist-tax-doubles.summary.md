# Porto Santo Doubles Tourist Tax to Four Euros

## Tax Increase Approved

Porto Santo municipal council has **approved doubling** the tourist tax from two to four euros per overnight stay. The measure aims to strengthen the municipality's financial capacity to respond to growing demands from tourism activity.

## Funding Sustainability and Development

The increased revenue will support necessary investments for sustainability and development of the island. Funds will be directed toward urban cleaning, maintenance of public spaces, environmental protection, infrastructure **improvement**, and destination qualification.

## Balancing Growth with Local Needs

Mayor Nuno Batista states the tax increase is **essential to accompany** tourism growth without compromising residents' quality of life or preservation of natural resources. Porto Santo faces structural challenges despite tourism's importance to the local economy.

The municipality continues to receive **minimal State transfers** compared to other regions, limiting investment capacity. Officials emphasize that tourism must contribute to Porto Santo's sustainability and improve quality of life for permanent residents. With the duplicated tax, executives aim to reconcile tourism growth with territory preservation and public service quality.

:::callout
**Context**: Porto Santo remains the last municipality in Portugal for State transfers despite hosting thousands of tourists annually. This financial limitation affects the council's ability to invest in infrastructure and services needed to support tourism growth while maintaining residents' quality of life.
:::
