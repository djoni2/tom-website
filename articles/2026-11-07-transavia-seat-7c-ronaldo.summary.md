# Transavia Retires Seat 7C on Funchal Flights for Ronaldo

The airline Transavia has paid an unusual tribute to **[Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo)**. The low-cost carrier announced on social media that it is **symbolically retiring seat 7C** on its flights to Funchal. The seat number refers to the shirt number that went with the Madeiran player through his career. The tribute follows the end of his career with Portugal's national team.

The post shows a **Cristiano Ronaldo shirt** placed on that very seat. It was shared by the account Transavia France.

> CR7 hangs up his boots, and our 7C seats are also being retired from flights to Funchal.

**Transavia**, on social media

## Ronaldo Ends 23 Years With Portugal's National Team at 41

The 41-year-old player announced **on Thursday** that he was ending his time with the national team. His international career lasted 23 years. dnoticias.pt calls it one of the **longest and most successful** careers in the history of national-team football.

## 234 Matches and 146 Goals Put Him at the Top

Ronaldo leaves the national team with **234 international matches** and 146 goals. These figures put him at the top of the lists for the most matches and the most goals for a national team.

## Still Playing for Al Nassr and Chasing 1,000 Career Goals

Ronaldo is not stopping completely. He still plays for **Al Nassr** in Saudi Arabia. One of his goals is to reach 1,000 goals in his professional career.

## Ryanair Posts One Word on 5 November, TAP Sends a Tribute

Not every airline reacted the same way. Ryanair replied to a post by the journalist **[Fabrizio Romano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabrizio_Romano)** about the end of Ronaldo's international career. The Irish airline wrote **a single word**: "Finally". The jab drew many reactions on social media.
