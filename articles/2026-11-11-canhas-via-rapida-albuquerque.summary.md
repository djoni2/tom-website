# Canhas To Get Expressway Access, Albuquerque Says

Miguel Albuquerque, the president of Madeira's regional government, said Wednesday that **Canhas will get access** to the Via Rápida expressway. He tied the project to an upcoming renegotiation of the region's public-private road partnerships.

Albuquerque gave no date for the work. He said only that the **renegotiation process** is starting.

## Pledge Comes At Ponta Do Sol Housing Handover

Albuquerque made the announcement on the sidelines of a ceremony in **Ponta do Sol**. He pointed to Canhas's recent expansion, saying the parish "has registered significant growth in housing and business."

He called the **planned connection** fundamental "to improving traffic flow and residents' quality of life."

## Thirty Low-Cost Homes Delivered At Mero VIII

The Ponta do Sol event itself was the handover of **30 controlled-cost homes** at the Mero VIII building. The source gives no further detail on the homes, such as their price or who received them.

## Renegotiation To Start Soon, No Timeline Given

Albuquerque linked the Canhas connection directly to the **renegotiation of** Madeira's public-private partnerships for roads.

> We have it, and in that renegotiation we will provide access here to the expressway for the parish of Canhas.

**Miguel Albuquerque**, President of Madeira's Regional Government

Asked when the connection would be built, Albuquerque said only that the **renegotiation was beginning**. He did not give a completion date or say how the new access would be built.
