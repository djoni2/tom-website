# Madeira's Workforce Reaches a Record 142,900 People

Madeira's active population reached **142,900 people** in the third quarter of 2026. That is 2.1% more than a year earlier, or 2,900 people.

According to the regional statistics office, DREM, this is **a new all-time high** in the current data series. It continues a run of successive highs seen since the first quarter of 2025.

Compared with the second quarter of 2026, the active population was **practically unchanged**, with 100 more people.

## Unemployment Rate Falls 1.3 Points to 4.0% in the Third Quarter

The **unemployment rate** in Madeira was estimated at 4.0% in the third quarter, the results of the Employment Survey show. That is 1.3 percentage points lower than a year earlier.

The rate was **0.5 points higher** than in the second quarter of 2026.

The rate for **men was higher**, at 4.1%, while the rate for women was 3.9%.

## Number of Employed People Rises by 4,700 to 137,200

The **employed population** stood at 137,200 people. That is 3.6% more than a year earlier, an increase of 4,700 people.

Compared with the second quarter, employment **fell by 0.4%**, or 600 people.

## Unemployed Population Drops 23.7% Over the Year to 5,700

The number of **unemployed people** totalled 5,700. That is 23.7% fewer than a year earlier, a drop of 1,800 people.

Compared with the second quarter of 2026, unemployment **rose by 13.7%**, or 700 people.

## Labour Underutilisation Affects 10,700 People, a Rate of 7.4%

**Labour underutilisation** covered 10,700 people. That is 17.2% fewer than a year earlier, or 2,200 people, but 5.4% more than in the previous quarter, or 600 people.

The **underutilisation rate** was estimated at 7.4%. It was 1.7 points lower than a year earlier and 0.4 points higher than in the previous quarter.

## 18,100 Employed People Worked From Home in the Third Quarter

Among the people in work, **18,100 worked from home**. That was 14.5% of employed women and 11.9% of employed men.

Another **2,600 people** were underemployed part-time, and 6,500 had a second job.

## Activity Rate of 62.4% Leaves 121,600 People Outside the Labour Force

The **activity rate** of people of working age, from 16 to 89, was estimated at 62.4%. That is 0.6 points higher than a year earlier and 0.2 points lower than in the previous quarter.

The activity rate **for women** was 59.0%, 7.2 points below the rate for men, at 66.2%.

The **inactive population** was estimated at 121,600 people. It fell by 0.5% over the year, or 600 people, but rose by 0.7% on the previous quarter, or 800 people.

## Madeira and Algarve Share Portugal's Lowest Rate as National Figure Hits 5.5%

In Portugal as a whole, the unemployment rate was **5.5%** in the third quarter. That is 0.3 points lower than a year earlier and 0.2 points higher than in the previous quarter.

Madeira and the Algarve had the **lowest rates** of all regions, both at 4.0%. The highest rates were in the Península de Setúbal (6.3%), the Norte (6.2%) and Grande Lisboa (6.0%).

Over the year, Madeira saw **the largest fall** in unemployment among Portugal's NUTS II regions, at 1.3 points. The Norte was the only region where the rate rose compared with a year earlier, by 0.1 points.

Over the quarter, the rate **rose by 0.5 points** in both Madeira and the Norte. The biggest quarterly rises were in the Alentejo (0.8 points) and the Centro (0.6 points).
