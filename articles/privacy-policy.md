## 1. Introduction

This is the Privacy Policy of Times of Madeira, reachable under the internet address https://www.timesofmadeira.com

## 2. What Information Do We Collect?

Our website uses Hyvor Talk ([https://talk.hyvor.com/](https://talk.hyvor.com/)), a commenting platform that helps us facilitate discussions among our users. We value your privacy and want to ensure that you are aware of how we collect and use your data when you use Hyvor Talk.

**Data Collection**

Hyvor Talk collects certain information from users when they post comments on our website. This information includes:

- **User name:** The name that the user selects to identify themselves.
- **Email address (optional):** The user's email address, which they can provide if they want to receive notifications about their comments.
- **IP address:** The user's IP address, which is a unique identifier for their device and allows us to protect the comments from spam.
- **Comment content:** The text that the user posts in their comment.

## 3. Embedded Content From Other Websites

Content on Times of Madeira may contain embedded elements such as videos, posts, or images from external sites. This website specifically loads scripts from **Meta (Facebook)**, **X (Twitter)**, and **Instagram** to display embedded posts and widgets. These providers may set cookies, track your browsing activity, and collect data about your visit, including if you are logged into their service, regardless of whether you interact with an embed directly.

You can review each provider's privacy policy here:

- **[Meta (Facebook) Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)**
- **[X (Twitter) Privacy Policy](https://x.com/en/privacy)**
- **[Instagram Privacy Policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy/)**

Other external content embedded on individual articles, for example YouTube videos or third-party widgets, functions similarly to visiting that external site directly, and may involve additional tracking by that site.

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics and Google AdSense to understand how visitors use the site and to serve relevant advertising. These services use first-party and third-party cookies to collect data about your visit.

**Google Analytics**

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and understand how visitors interact with our articles. Google Analytics collects information such as your approximate location, derived from your IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is processed in accordance with the **[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)**. You can opt out by installing the **[Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)**.

**Google AdSense**

We use Google AdSense to display advertising on this website. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising via **[Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)** or **[www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/)**.

**Cookie Consent**

When you visit this website, a cookie banner may let you accept or manage non-essential cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site and reloading the page.

## 5. Push Notifications

With your consent, this website uses **OneSignal** ([https://onesignal.com/](https://onesignal.com/)) to send you browser push notifications about new articles. If you allow notifications, OneSignal assigns your browser a subscription identifier and processes your IP address (used to derive your approximate location and timezone), browser and device type, and your interactions with the notifications we send (for example, whether you clicked one). You can withdraw this consent at any time through your browser's site settings, which immediately stops any further notifications. You can review OneSignal's privacy policy **[here](https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy)**.

## 6. Disclosing of Information to Outside Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

## 7. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page.
