# IPMA Issues New Weather Warnings for Tuesday and Wednesday

Portugal's weather service, **IPMA**, has issued new yellow warnings for Madeira and Porto Santo. The agency expects weather conditions to worsen again between Tuesday, 10 November, and Wednesday, 11 November.

The new alerts follow a night of **orange warnings**. Madeira has now been downgraded to yellow for rain and wind, though forecasters say more bad weather is on the way.

## Rain and Wind Alerts Take Effect at 21:00 Tuesday

The rain and wind warnings start at **21:00 on Tuesday**. Rain may be strong and persistent at times.

Wind could bring gusts of up to **75 km/h**. That threshold applies to the North and South coasts and to Porto Santo.

The wind warning ends at **10:00 on Wednesday**. The rain warning runs longer, lasting until midday.

## Gusts May Reach 95 Km/H in the Mountains

In Madeira's mountainous areas, IPMA expects wind gusts of up to **95 km/h**. That is notably stronger than the 75 km/h forecast for the coasts.

## South Coast Faces a Sea Warning With 4.5-Metre Waves

IPMA has also issued a yellow warning for sea conditions on the **South coast**. It runs from 07:00 to 13:00 on Wednesday, with waves from the West and Southwest reaching between 4 and 4.5 metres.

## North Coast and Porto Santo Get a Sea Alert for Monday

A separate update adds sea warnings for **Monday, 9 November**. The North coast is expected to see waves from the West and Northwest of 4 to 4.5 metres.

Porto Santo faces waves of the **same height** from the Northwest.

All the new warnings are linked to the passage of **depression Bruna**. IPMA says it will keep bringing rain, strong wind and rough seas to the archipelago over the coming days.

## Depression Bruna Has Already Caused at Least 111 Occurrences

Bruna had already caused at least **111 occurrences** across the region, according to today's print edition of DIÁRIO, Madeira's daily newspaper.

Five people were left **homeless**, and the storm disrupted air and sea links as well as sports activities, according to the same report.

## Civil Protection's Count Climbs to 145 Incidents

Separately, Madeira's Civil Protection service has counted **145 occurrences** linked to the bad weather. Fallen trees and floods were the most common incidents.

**Funchal** recorded the most, with 66 occurrences. Santa Cruz followed with 31.

Flight disruption at Madeira Airport has also continued through the week. **[Cancellations climbed to 37](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/madeira-airport-cancellations-rise-to-37-storm-bruna)** on Saturday alone, as storm Bruna intensified.
