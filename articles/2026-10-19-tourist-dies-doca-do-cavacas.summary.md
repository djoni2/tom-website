# Swiss Tourist Dies at Doca do Cavacas Bathing Complex in Funchal

By **Mat**

A Swiss man died on the afternoon of 19 October at the **Doca do Cavacas** bathing complex in Funchal. He was about 60 years old.

He was found in the water in **cardiorespiratory arrest**. The alarm was raised at around 16:00.

## Lifeguards Reacted at Once

Lifeguards at the site **helped him straight away**. Emergency services were then called in quickly.

Bruno Teles, the **Captain of the Port** of Funchal, confirmed this to the newspaper DIÁRIO.

## Emergency Services Sent

The rapid medical team **EMIR** came to the complex. So did an ambulance from the Portuguese Red Cross and the local Maritime Police.

The **Madeiran Volunteer Firefighters** also came to help. That is according to Válter Ferreira, deputy commander at the Regional Civil Protection Service.

In total, 4 units and **11 responders** were at the scene.

## Death Confirmed at the Site

The teams tried to **bring him back**, but their efforts failed. His death was confirmed at the scene.

The report does **not give a cause** of death. It also does not name the man.
