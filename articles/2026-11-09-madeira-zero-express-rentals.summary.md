# Madeira Had No 24-Hour Rentals as Portugal's Rate Hit 7%

Not a single rental listing in Madeira disappeared from the market within 24 hours in the third quarter of 2026. That is according to new data from **idealista**, the real estate marketplace.

Nationally, the picture was very different. About **7% of rental ads** in Portugal vanished in under a day during the same period, idealista said.

## Madeira and São Miguel See No Rentals Gone in a Day

Madeira was one of only four districts and islands, out of **18 analysed**, with zero so-called "express rentals". The others were Beja, Guarda and the island of São Miguel.

idealista linked this to a **mix of factors**: weaker demand, less attractive locations, poor market positioning and mismatched prices.

## One in 14 Rental Ads Nationwide Vanish Within 24 Hours

Across Portugal, roughly **one in 14** rental listings disappeared before many interested tenants could even arrange a viewing. idealista said this rate held steady from the previous quarter.

idealista describes itself as **southern Europe's property marketplace**. Its data team, idealista/data, compiled the figures from the platform and other public and private sources.

## Setúbal and Viana do Castelo Top Districts at 17%

At district level, Setúbal and Viana do Castelo had the **fastest-moving rental markets**. In both, 17% of listings were gone within 24 hours.

Castelo Branco followed at 13%, then Leiria at 12%. **Évora and Vila Real** both posted 11%, while Coimbra and Santarém both reached 10%. Braga stood at 9%.

## Lisbon's District Rate Stays Low at Just 6%

**Lisbon** had one of the lowest district rates in the country, at 6%. Porto was just behind, at 5%.

**Aveiro and Faro** also stayed below 10%, both at 8%. Viseu followed at 7%.

## Viana do Castelo Leads Cities With 23% Turnover

Looking only at Portugal's largest cities, **Viana do Castelo** had the highest share of express rentals, at 23%. Leiria followed at 18%.

**Vila Real** reached 17%, Évora 13%, and Castelo Branco and Setúbal both 11%. Coimbra posted 10% and Braga 8%.

## Funchal Ties With Beja and Ponta Delgada for Lowest Turnover

At city level, **Funchal** also recorded zero express rentals. Only Beja and Ponta Delgada matched that result.

By contrast, Lisbon and Porto each posted just **4%**. Aveiro followed at 5%.

idealista's data comes from its own listings platform, combined with other **public and private sources**. The figures cover the third quarter of 2026, the Portuguese summer months.
