# FNAC Madeira Reopens Nov 19 With João Pedro Pais Concert

FNAC is celebrating **20 years** on Madeira with the reopening of its store at MadeiraShopping. The shop has moved into a new gallery on Piso 1, in a fully renovated space. The reopening kicks off **four days of events**, running from November 19 to 22. The schedule mixes regional and national artists with activities for different generations.

## New Store Opens With Over 33,000 Physical Items

The renovated shop now carries **more than 33,000** physical items. The range covers literature, music, computing, photography, technology, stationery, musical instruments and games. Beyond the shelves, the store keeps a ticket office and repair and technical assistance services. It also offers the benefits tied to the **FNAC Card**, including membership and financing options.

## João Pedro Pais And João Inácio Open Celebrations At 18:30

The celebrations start on **November 19 at 18:30**, with a pre-opening event. The guests are singer João Pedro Pais and violinist João Inácio.

## 2020 Festival Da Canção Winner Elisa Takes The Stage

The next day, it is the turn of Elisa, a singer from Madeira, to perform on the new store's stage. She won the **Festival da Canção** in 2020.

## Four-Day Programme Ends With Jam Session And Story Hour

The programme continues on **November 21** with a jam session by Projeto Melro Preto. It closes the next day with the return of the "Hora do Conto" storytelling initiative, led by Vanusca and the Amigas da Pequenada. A performance by the **Duo Bruna e Luís** follows.

## New Fórum FNAC Settles Into MadeiraShopping's Piso 1

Besides the renovated retail space, the store now has a **Fórum FNAC**. It is designed to host meetings between artists, authors, creators and the public, reinforcing the brand's cultural role in the region. In a statement, Nuno Luz, general manager of FNAC DARTY Ibéria, pointed to the ties built with the Madeiran community over **two decades**.

> Along these two decades, we have tried to be much more than a commercial space: we want to be a place of discovery, meeting and a stage for culture, bringing artists, authors and the public together.

**Nuno Luz**, general manager of FNAC DARTY Ibéria

He said the new store offers better conditions to continue that work, supporting regional and national **cultural creation**.
