# Madeira Faces Four Days of Rain, Wind and Big Waves

Madeira is preparing for several days of **bad weather**. Forecasters expect heavy rain, thunderstorms, strong winds and rough seas.

Gusts could reach **110 km/h** in the mountains. Waves could reach four metres.

The Regional Civil Protection Service issued **a public warning** on Thursday, 5 November. It is based on forecasts from IPMA, Portugal's weather institute.

## A Complex Low Centred Near the Azores Drives the Bad Weather

The cause is a **frontal system** linked to a complex area of low pressure. The low is centred over the Azores region.

It is expected to affect Madeira until **at least Monday**, 9 November.

## Heaviest Rain Expected From Saturday, 7 November

Showers are expected from Thursday. They will be at times heavy, with thunder, mainly on **the south coast** and in the mountains.

The rain could become **more intense and persistent** over the weekend, on 7 and 8 November. Heavy showers and thunderstorms remain possible until Monday.

## Yellow Warnings Start at 12:00 on Saturday Across Madeira and Porto Santo

IPMA updated and **extended its warnings** on Thursday. The yellow warnings were in force from Wednesday and were due to end on Friday. They now run into Saturday and Sunday.

They now also cover **the north coast** and Porto Santo, as well as the south coast and the mountains of Madeira.

The most critical period is expected on **Saturday and early Sunday**, according to DNoticias.

A yellow rain warning applies to all four areas **from 12:00 on Saturday** until 03:00 on Sunday. IPMA expects rain that is at times heavy and persistent, possibly with thunder.

A yellow wind warning runs from 12:00 on Saturday **until 00:00 on Sunday**. It covers the north and south coasts, Porto Santo and the mountains.

The south coast and the mountains were also under rain warnings on Thursday, **from 09:00 to 15:00**. Another one runs on Friday, from 00:00 to 15:00.

DNoticias reports that the warnings **could still be raised** above the current level.

## Gusts of Up to 110 km/h Forecast for the Mountains

The wind is expected to **grow stronger from Saturday**. It will blow from the south and southwest.

Gusts could reach **90 km/h** on the north and south coasts of Madeira and in Porto Santo. In the mountains, they could reach 110 km/h.

The wind should **slowly ease** from Sunday afternoon. On Monday, gusts of up to 70 km/h are still possible in the mountains.

## Southwest Waves Expected on the South Coast on 8 and 9 November

Conditions at sea will also be **rough**. The sea is expected to get rougher from Saturday.

Waves from the southwest could reach **four metres** on the south coast on 8 and 9 November.

On the north coast, waves from the northwest of **one to two metres** are expected. They should slowly grow to three to four metres.

On Wednesday, Times of Madeira reported that the bad weather has [cancelled Lobo Marinho trips](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/bad-weather-cancels-lobo-marinho-trips-on-7-and-8-november) on **7 and 8 November**.

## Civil Protection Lists Five Risks, From Falling Trees to Coastal Flooding

Civil Protection warns of **falling trees and branches**. It also warns of flooding in urban areas and problems with drainage.

Walls and **slopes could collapse**. The sea could also wash over coastal areas.

People should **avoid unnecessary travel**, especially to exposed areas or areas hit by the bad weather.

Drivers should **drive defensively** and slow down. Sheets of water could form on the roads.

The authorities also advise people to **clear drains** and secure loose objects. They ask for extra care near the coast, in wooded areas and in the mountains.

Civil Protection asks people to **respect closures** and access limits in signposted areas. It also asks them to follow weather updates and the instructions of the authorities.
