# Madeira Shuts Schools Wednesday Morning After Red Alert

The Regional Government of Madeira has ordered all schools closed on Wednesday morning, until **13:00**. Officials announced the decision in a note sent to the press. The government said the closure is a **prevention and safety measure** for the population, citing a warning from Portugal's meteorological institute, IPMA.

## Closure Runs Until 13:00 on Wednesday

Schools across the island will stay shut for **the whole morning**, with classes expected to resume only after 13:00. The order applies to the entire region, and the note gave no exception for individual municipalities or school levels.

## IPMA's Red Warning Covers Rain and Wind From Tuesday Night

IPMA has issued a **red warning** for heavy rain and strong wind, covering the night of Tuesday into the early hours of Wednesday. The storm is linked to Depression Charles. Earlier in the week, Charles brought forecasts of **[130 km/h gusts](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/depression-charles-130-kmh-gusts-madeira)** and over 100 millimeters of rain to the region.

## Government Urges Madeira Residents to Stay Indoors

Non-essential public services may also allow **remote work** during Wednesday morning, according to the note.

> Residents should avoid leaving their homes, except in situations of extreme necessity, especially during the periods of heaviest rain and wind.

**Regional Government of Madeira**, in its note to the press
