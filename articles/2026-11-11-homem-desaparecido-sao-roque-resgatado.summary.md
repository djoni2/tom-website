# Elderly Man Rescued Alive After Stream Floods Garage in Funchal

A 78-year-old man who had been reported missing in the Funchal parish of São Roque was **rescued alive** on Wednesday. A stream had overflowed near his home, according to dnoticias.pt.

The man was in his **garage** when the stream, known locally as a "ribeiro," burst its banks on Caminho da Igreja Velha. The flooding followed **heavy rain** linked to the passage of depression Charles over Madeira.

## 78-Year-Old Pulled From Garage Without Serious Injuries

Crews from the **Bombeiros Voluntários Madeirenses**, the Madeiran volunteer fire service, carried out the rescue. Officers from the **Polícia de Segurança Pública** assisted them at the scene.

The man appeared to be **in good condition**, with no serious injuries reported. He will now be taken to hospital for a more detailed assessment of his health.

## Depression Charles Also Forecast to Bring 130 Km/h Gusts

The flooding came as **depression Charles** moved across Madeira, bringing intense rain to the island. The system had prompted **[orange-level weather warnings](https://www.timesofmadeira.com/depression-charles-130-kmh-gusts-madeira)** from Portugal's weather institute, IPMA, which also forecast gusts of up to 130 km/h in high ground.
