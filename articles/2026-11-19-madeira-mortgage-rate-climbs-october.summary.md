# Madeira's Mortgage Rate Keeps Rising in October

The implicit interest rate on housing loans in Madeira **continued to rise** in October 2026, according to data from Portugal's National Statistics Institute (INE), released by the Regional Directorate of Statistics of Madeira (DREM). The rate reached **3.340%**, up 0.052 percentage points from September.

## Implicit Rate Stands 0.136 Points Above Last Year

The implicit rate is calculated from the stock of **housing loan contracts** already in force in the Region, not only new loans signed in the month. A year earlier, in October 2025, it stood at 3.204%.

That puts October's reading **0.136 percentage points higher** than 12 months before.

## Average Monthly Payment Rises to 418 Euros

The average monthly installment due on housing loan contracts in Madeira rose to **418 euros** in October, 2 euros more than in September. Interest made up 204 euros of that amount, up 4 euros from the previous month, while the amortization portion fell 2 euros, to 214 euros.

## Outstanding Mortgage Debt Grows to 74,344 Euros

The average outstanding capital on housing loan contracts in the region also kept climbing, reaching **74,344 euros** in October, up from 73,732 euros in September. A year earlier, in October 2025, the average balance owed was 69,860 euros.

Over the past 12 months, the average balance owed in Madeira has **grown by 4,484 euros**.

## National Rate Trails Madeira's, at 3.231 Percent

Nationally, the implicit interest rate for housing loans stood at **3.231%** in October. That is 0.037 percentage points higher than in September, a smaller rise than in Madeira. The national rate stayed below the Region's throughout the month.

The average installment due across Portugal rose to 427 euros. The average outstanding capital grew to 81,471 euros, up from 80,843 euros in September. Interest payments nationally increased 4 euros, to 217 euros, while the amortization portion held **unchanged at 210 euros**.

## Payments Climbed 24 Euros From a Year Earlier

In Madeira, the average installment due was 394 euros in October 2025. A year later, it stands at 418 euros, an increase of **24 euros** over the 12-month period.

Rising borrowing costs add to a housing market **already under strain** in the Region. A recent idealista study found that families in Funchal need 96% of their income to cover a mortgage payment, the highest share among Portugal's 20 biggest cities.
