# IFCN Keeps 41 Madeira Trails Closed After Storm Charles

Madeira's **41 classified hiking trails** will stay closed through Thursday, the Institute of Forests and Nature Conservation (IFCN) announced on Wednesday. The decision follows the effects of **storm Charles**, which left several trail accesses blocked.

## Closure Holds Through Thursday, IFCN Says

The IFCN said the storm left access to the trails **conditioned** in several places. Instability in the ground also remains in parts of the terrain.

> The measure's priority is to safeguard the safety of all residents and visitors, preventing possible risk situations during nature and mountain activities.

**Institute of Forests and Nature Conservation (IFCN)**

The institute said it will **keep monitoring** how the situation evolves. It will assess the condition of each trail to decide when it is safe to reopen them.

The IFCN also asked the public for **understanding and cooperation**. It recommended following the authorities' guidance and respecting signage and existing access restrictions.
