# Couple Invests 500,000 Euros in New Gym in Ponta do Sol

By **Mat**

Alex Sampaio and his wife have opened a **new gym** in Ponta do Sol. The project is worth about 500,000 euros.

The project came after their **old space** became too small for the demand. Their goal is to get more people to exercise.

## Solar Power and a View

Sampaio says the new building was planned to be **"self-sustaining"**. Solar panels should bring its energy use to zero during the summer.

The front of the building is **made of glass**. It lets in natural light and gives a view of the scenery.

## Clients of All Ages

The team has **eight exercise professionals** and one nutritionist. The number of people who signed up at the start surprised the owners.

> "We were not expecting this demand."

**Alex Sampaio**, gym owner

The old gym already had **clients up to 85** years old. The new one wants to serve people of all ages and fitness levels.

The team also teaches classes in **several places** across the municipality. This is part of a town council programme that also targets older residents. Sampaio says these classes are in very high demand.
