# SIGA Launches New Route 560 From Funchal to Cristo Rei

A new SIGA bus route will connect Funchal to the Cristo Rei area of Caniço starting next Monday, November 9. Route 560 is designed to serve a part of Santa Cruz municipality that has seen fast **residential and tourist growth**. According to the official statement, the goal is to improve **accessibility and mobility** for people living there.

## Route 560 Begins Monday With Eight Daily Trips

The new line starts running on **Monday, November 9**. On weekdays during the school term, it will make **eight trips a day** in each direction, with services guaranteed both ways.

## Service Drops to Seven Trips During School Holidays

Outside term time, the schedule eases back a little. The route will run **seven times a day** on weekdays during school holidays, still covering both directions.

## Weekends and Holidays See Just Two Return Trips

On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, route 560 is scaled down further. It will make just **two trips a day** in each direction on those days.

## Bus Links Funchal's Old Town to Café Jardim in Caniço

The route starts near the **cable car station** in Funchal's Zona Velha, the Old Town. From there it runs along Rua Brigadeiro Oudinot and Campo da Barca, then joins the **Via Rápida** to the Garajau exit. It continues along Estrada do Garajau and Estrada do Cristo Rei, ending near **Café Jardim**, close to Quinta Splendida.

## Some Trips Extend to Reis Magos and Edifício Ventura

A number of the route's runs will go further, continuing to **Reis Magos**. Those extended trips also cover the Picos area and the **Edifício Ventura** building.

## New Line Comes From This Year's Addendum With Operator CAM

Route 560 was created through an addendum to the public road passenger transport concession contract. The addendum was signed **this year** between the Regional Government, through the Secretariat for Equipment and Infrastructure, and operator **CAM**. As part of the process, the regional transport authority **IMT, IP-RAM** worked with the operator to review and adapt the wider network, which made the new connection possible. The government says the new line responds to **mobility needs** identified in that part of Santa Cruz municipality.

## Addendum Also Increases Aerobus Runs to the Airport

The same contract addendum covers other changes to Madeira and Porto Santo's public transport network. It increases the number of **Aerobus** trips, which connect **Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport** to Funchal. It also reinforces service on the **Machico-Santa Cruz-Funchal** route, and improves school transport links in the municipalities of Santana and Porto Santo. With these measures, the Regional Government says it wants public transport to keep pace with **rising demand**.

Timetables and stops for route 560 are published on SIGA's information channels, at **[siga.madeira.gov.pt/horarios/6005](https://siga.madeira.gov.pt/horarios/6005)**.
