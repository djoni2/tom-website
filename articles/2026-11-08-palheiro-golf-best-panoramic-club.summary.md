# Palheiro Golf Claims Top World Panoramic Award Again

Palheiro Golfe has won the **Best Panoramic Golf Club** award at the World Golf Awards 2026. It is the **third year** in a row that the Madeira course has taken the title, after wins in 2024 and 2025.

Clube de Golfe do Santo da Serra, also on the island, picked up its own honor at the same ceremony. Its **Par 3 hole** was named the best in the world for 2026, also for the third consecutive year.

## World Golf Awards Hold 13th Edition in Egypt

The winners were announced on Saturday, 8 November, during the **13th edition** of the World Golf Awards. The ceremony took place at the Madinaty Golf Club in Egypt.

It was the **first time** the international event was held in Africa.

## Palheiro Golf Wins Panoramic Prize for Third Year Running

The award recognizes the panoramic qualities of Palheiro Golfe, set in the **Quinta do Palheiro** estate. The course sits at around 500 meters of altitude, with views over the Atlantic Ocean and Madeira's mountainous landscape.

It is the club's **third straight win** in the category, following its 2024 and 2025 titles.

## Championship Course Rises 500 Meters Above the Atlantic

The championship course has **18 holes** and a par of 72, built into pine forest, botanical woodland and subtropical vegetation. **Cabell B. Robinson**, a golf course architect, designed the layout.

The course uses the property's rugged terrain, with ridges and deep valleys that challenge players of different skill levels. It sits next to the five-star Casa Velha do Palheiro hotel, a member of the **Relais & Châteaux** association, about ten minutes from Funchal.

## Santo da Serra's Par 3 Hole Wins World Prize Since 2024

Santo da Serra's Par 3 hole won the "World's Best Par 3 Hole 2026" category. This global prize recognizes a single standout hole on a golf course. The hole has now won the category **every year since 2024**.

Last year's edition had already brought a **record number of distinctions** to the region in a single year. Madeira was named Best Emerging Destination, a title it also held in 2019. Santo da Serra also won Best Sustainable Water Catchment Course System in the World.

## Portugal Named World's Best Golf Destination in 2026

Beyond Madeira's two wins, Portugal was named **Best Golf Destination** in the world. It is the main category of the awards. The source reports Portugal has been its biggest winner across all 13 editions held so far.

Portugal also took three other world-level prizes. Costa de Prata won **Best Emerging Golf Destination**. Terras da Comporta won Best Golf Eco-Facility, and Quinta do Lago was named **Best Golf Academy** in the world.

## Portugal Wins Nine More Awards in Europe and at Home

At the European level, Portugal collected **five awards**:

- **Tee Times** - Best Golf Tour Operator
- **PGA Aroeira** - Best Eco-Friendly Golf Facility
- **Palmares Ocean Living & Golf** - Best Club House
- **Quinta do Lago** - Best Golf Resort
- **Vilamoura Golf** - Best Golf Development

In the national categories, **four more prizes** went to:

- **Monte Rei** - Best Golf Course in Portugal
- **Praia D'El Rey** Marriott Golf & Beach Resort - Best Golf Hotel
- **InGolf** - Best Inbound Tour Operator
- **Tee Times** - Best Outbound Tour Operator
