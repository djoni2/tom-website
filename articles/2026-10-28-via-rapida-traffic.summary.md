# Via Rápida Carries 40,500 Vehicles a Day Through September

Madeira's Via Rápida carried an average of **40,500 vehicles a day** between January and September 2026. The island's express roads, the Vias Expresso, carried 8,200 a day.

The figures come from the Regional Roads Directorate (DRE). They were **released on Wednesday**.

## Traffic Still Growing

Traffic on the Via Rápida rose **1.4% year on year**, compared with the same period of 2025. On the express roads, the article gives growth of 2.1% in one passage and 2.6% in another.

Cars and other **light vehicles drove** most of the increase. Their traffic grew 1.4% on the Via Rápida and 2.6% on the express roads.

Heavy vehicles also rose, by 1.8% and 2.1%. But they make up **only about 2%** of all traffic, so their effect on the total is small.

## Growth Slows in Summer

The third quarter shows a **clear slowdown**. Between July and September, traffic on the Via Rápida stayed flat. On the express roads it rose just 0.7%.

That is a change from the **second quarter**. Then, traffic grew 1.7% on the Via Rápida and 2.6% on the express roads.

The article says this points to **less momentum** on both concession road networks. It does not give a reason for the slowdown.
