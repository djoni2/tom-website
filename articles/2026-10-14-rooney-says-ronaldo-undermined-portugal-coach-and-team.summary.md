# Rooney Says Ronaldo Undermined Portugal's Coach and Team

Wayne Rooney has criticised
[Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo) for the way
he left the Portugal squad. The former England forward says the Madeiran **disrespected the coach**,
his teammates and the Portuguese Football Federation.

Rooney spoke on the podcast 'Stick to United'. He played **205 games** next to
Ronaldo at Manchester United, where they shared a dressing room between 2004
and 2009.

> "In my opinion, it was wrong in every sense. To undermine his coach and his teammates the way he did was very wrong. He undermined the Federation."

**[Wayne Rooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_Rooney)**, former England international

:::callout
**Who he is**: Rooney is Manchester United's all-time top scorer, with 253
goals, and he won five league titles at the club. He later worked as a manager.
:::

He also said it would be **a shame** if this ended Ronaldo's Portugal career.
In his view, the forward is not handling the end of his career well, and that
is uncomfortable to watch.

Ronaldo left the camp on **30 September**, before Nations League matches
against Denmark and Norway. He blamed a **breach of trust** by the coach,
[Jorge Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Jesus).

In a statement on 6 October, Ronaldo apologised to fans and teammates. He said
he would be available again for the national team once he has served **his
suspension**. He added that he would need to be fit and scoring at his club.

The report does **not say** how long that suspension will be, and it carries
no response from Jorge Jesus or from the federation.
