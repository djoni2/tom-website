# Caniçal Port Receives 27 Million Euro Investment for Expansion

By **Mat**

## Investment Details

The Commercial Port of Caniçal will receive **27 million euros** to expand its facilities and double cargo handling capacity. The investment includes 10 million euros for landfill expansion and funds for breakwater rehabilitation from the Madeira 2030 framework.

## Capacity Increase

Three new electric cranes, representing a 14 million euro investment, will allow the port to double its cargo movement capacity. Cargo discharged in Madeira increased **35%** between 2020 and 2025, with container traffic growing from 52,000 to 72,000 annually. The cranes arrived in mid-September and the landfill expansion is nearly complete.

> "The first concern is not to increase charges"

**[Miguel Albuquerque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miguel_Albuquerque)**, President of the Regional Government

## Economic Impact

The port handles **95%** of cargo that arrives in Madeira, showing its critical role in the island's economy.

Beyond the new cranes, the project includes investments in electricity, gas, and sanitation. It also includes technology including **AI for container monitoring** to collect data on cargo movements and obtain indicators for regional economic activity analysis. The next phase will expand the landfill in the port's eastern zone and rehabilitate the main breakwater to further increase operational capacity.
